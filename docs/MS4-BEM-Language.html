CHAPTER 01 · MASTERY OF LANGUAGE

What Is Mastery of Language?

This part of the BEM examination assesses your ability to use English grammar, vocabulary, word forms and language conventions correctly.

🧠

Morphology

Use correct verb forms, word formation, prefixes, suffixes and grammatical structures.

🔄

Syntax

Build meaningful sentences, transform structures and connect ideas correctly.

🔤

Mechanics & Sounds

Practise spelling, capital letters, punctuation, word stress and pronunciation.

📊 Mastery of Language Is Worth 7 Points

In the BEM examination framework supplied for this project, the Mastery of Language section is allocated 7 points. The practice activities below help you revise language skills through short exercises and sentence transformations.
