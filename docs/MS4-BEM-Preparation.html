✦ SMART EXAMINATION STRATEGIES ✦

Three Golden Tips for BEM Success

Use these simple strategies whenever you practise an examination.

🔍

Read Before You Answer

Read the instructions carefully. Underline key words and look for information in the text before writing your answers.

⏱️

Manage Your Time

Do not spend too much time on one difficult question. Complete the easier activities first and leave time to review your work.

✍️

Check Your Writing

Before submitting, check capital letters, punctuation, spelling, verb forms and the organisation of your paragraph.
