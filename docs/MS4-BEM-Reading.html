CHAPTER 01 · READING COMPREHENSION

What Does Reading Comprehension Mean?

Reading comprehension is the ability to understand, interpret and use information from a written text.

🔎

Understand

Identify the topic, main idea and important details in a passage.

🎯

Find Information

Locate names, dates, places, events and explanations to answer questions accurately.

🧠

Interpret

Understand vocabulary in context, identify references and make reasonable inferences.

📊 Reading Is Worth 7 Points

According to the 2018 BEM English examination construction guide, Reading Comprehension includes two comprehension activities and one lexis activity.

Activity 1: 3 points · Activity 2: 2 points · Lexis: 2 points

7/20

CHAPTER 02 · READING STRATEGIES

The Four Reading Superpowers

Use these strategies to understand passages more effectively and answer examination questions accurately.

01

Skimming

Read quickly to understand the general topic and main idea.

💡 Ask: What is this text mainly about?
02

Scanning

Search for specific information such as names, dates, places and numbers.

💡 Ask: Where can I find this information?
03

Detailed Reading

Read carefully to understand relationships, explanations and important facts.

💡 Ask: What exactly does this sentence mean?
04

Inference

Use clues in the passage to understand ideas that are not stated directly.

💡 Ask: What can I understand from these clues?
Reading method: First, skim the passage. Next, read the questions. Then, scan for the relevant information. Finally, reread carefully before answering.
CHAPTER 03 · BEM QUESTION TYPES

Discover the Reading Activities

These are the main reading activity types identified in the 2018 BEM guide, illustrated with simple examples.

01 · Multiple Choice

Choose the correct answer from the proposed options.

Text: Sara joined a charity club to help poor families.

Question: Why did Sara join the club?
a) To travel abroad
b) To help poor families
c) To buy new clothes
b) To help poor families.

02 · True / False / Not Mentioned

Decide whether each statement agrees with the text, contradicts it or is not discussed.

Text: Karim visited Constantine in July.

1. Karim visited Constantine.
2. He travelled in December.
3. He travelled with his brother.
1. True.
2. False — he travelled in July.
3. Not mentioned.

03 · Open Questions

Answer questions beginning with who, what, where, when, why or how.

Text: Lina planted trees to make her neighbourhood greener.

Question: Why did Lina plant trees?
To make her neighbourhood greener.

04 · Information Transfer

Complete a table, chart or fact file with information from the text.

Text: The Eiffel Tower is in Paris. It was completed in 1889.

Complete:
Location: ______
Completion year: ______
Location: Paris.
Completion year: 1889.

05 · Matching

Match headings with paragraphs, dates with events or questions with answers.

Match:
1. 1889 → a. Location
2. Paris → b. Completion year
1 → b
2 → a

06 · Main Idea / Best Title

Choose the title or sentence that best summarises the passage.

Text: Pupils cleaned their school, planted flowers and recycled paper.

Choose:
a) A Football Match
b) Caring for Our School
c) A Family Holiday
b) Caring for Our School.

07 · Pronoun Reference

Identify the person, place or thing represented by a pronoun.

Text: Amira is a talented pupil. She enjoys reading.

Question: What does "She" refer to?
"She" refers to Amira.

08 · Synonyms and Opposites

Find words with similar or opposite meanings.

Text: Yasmine is a generous girl. She always helps people.

Question: Find a word meaning "willing to give".
Generous.

09 · Listing Information

Identify several details, examples, similarities or differences from the text.

Text: Volunteers collected clothes, prepared meals and cleaned the park.

Question: List two activities.
Any two:
Collected clothes.
Prepared meals.
Cleaned the park.

10 · Inference

Use clues in the text to understand something that is not stated directly.

Text: The pupils carried umbrellas and their shoes became wet.

Question: What was the weather probably like?
It was probably raining. The umbrellas and wet shoes provide clues.
Important: The BEM guide recommends that comprehension questions cover the whole text and follow the order in which the relevant information appears. This helps pupils work through the passage logically.
