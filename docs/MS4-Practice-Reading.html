⭐ A Smart Reader's Secret

You do not need to understand every word to understand a text. Begin with the general meaning. Read the questions carefully. Look for clues in the passage. When you finish, check your answers and discover what you can improve.

1 Read the title and predict the topic.
2 Read the passage for the general idea.
3 Read each question carefully.
4 Find evidence in the text.
5 Check your answers and improve.
