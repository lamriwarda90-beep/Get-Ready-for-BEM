🔬 MISSION 01 · DISCOVER

Meet the Silent Letters!

In English, some letters appear in spelling but disappear when we pronounce a word. Listen and discover three important silent-letter patterns.

K

The Invisible K

The letter K is usually silent at the beginning of words starting with kn. We pronounce the /n/ sound, but not the /k/ sound.

الحرف K لا ينطق عادة في بداية الكلمات التي تبدأ بـ kn. ننطق صوت n فقط.

W

The Invisible W

The letter W is silent at the beginning of many words starting with wr. We pronounce the /r/ sound, but not the /w/ sound.

الحرف W لا ينطق في بداية الكلمات التي تبدأ بـ wr. ننطق صوت r فقط.

B

The Invisible B

The letter B is silent in words such as climb, thumb and comb. It is often silent after m at the end of a word.

الحرف B لا ينطق في بعض الكلمات عندما يأتي بعد m في نهاية الكلمة.

🌟 More Silent Letter Secrets!

Silent letters are not limited to K, W and B. Explore these additional examples that are useful in everyday English.

🧠 The Golden Rule

A silent letter is a letter that appears in spelling but is not pronounced.

🟢 K: know, knee, knife

🟣 W: write, wrong, wrist

🟠 B: climb, thumb, comb

Other examples include silent T, H, L and S.

القاعدة الذهبية: الحرف الصامت هو حرف نكتبه في الكلمة لكن لا ننطقه. يجب أن نستمع إلى النطق الصحيح ولا نعتمد على الكتابة فقط.

A Journey of Knowledge and Kindness

🎯 Your Mission

First: Listen to the passage twice.

Next: Read the passage and find the words containing silent letters.

Finally: Classify the words according to their silent letters.

استمع إلى النص مرتين، ثم حدد الكلمات التي تحتوي على حروف صامتة، وبعدها صنفها حسب الحرف الصامت.

Last year, our teacher took us to visit an old castle. We wanted to know more about its history. We walked around the building and listened to an interesting story. Our guide shared his knowledge about the people who lived there.

The visit reminded me of my childhood. I used to write stories about brave heroes. Once, I hurt my knee while playing outside. My mother was always honest and kind. She taught me that it was wrong to hurt others.

Today, I believe good citizens should listen carefully and talk politely. We can design posters about helping others. We should never doubt the importance of kindness. Together, we can build a better community.
