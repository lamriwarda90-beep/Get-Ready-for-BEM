✦ BECOME A BETTER SPEAKER ✦

Three Golden Speaking Tips

Improve your pronunciation through simple daily habits.

👂

Listen Carefully

Pay attention to each sound. Notice the difference between similar English words.

🗣️

Repeat Aloud

Say each word several times. Practise slowly first, then speak more naturally.

🌟

Practise Every Day

A few minutes of regular pronunciation practice can help you improve.

🎤 Listen — Repeat — Speak!

Every sound you practise brings you closer to speaking English with more confidence. Do not be afraid of mistakes. Keep listening, repeating and enjoying your English journey!
