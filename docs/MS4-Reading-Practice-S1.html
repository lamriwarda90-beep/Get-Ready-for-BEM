MISSION 01 · LANDMARKS

The Great Wall of China: A Wonder of the World

Read carefully, then complete the comprehension challenges.

📖 Read the Text

The Great Wall of China is one of the most impressive historical landmarks in the world. It is located in northern China and stretches across mountains, valleys and deserts. Every year, millions of tourists visit this magnificent monument to discover its fascinating history and admire its breathtaking views.

The Great Wall was not built all at once. Its earliest sections date back to ancient times. In the third century BC, Emperor Qin Shi Huang ordered the connection of several existing walls to protect his empire. Later, other Chinese dynasties repaired and extended the structure. Many of the sections that tourists visit today were built during the Ming Dynasty between the 14th and 17th centuries.

The Great Wall is more than 21,000 kilometres long when all its known sections are counted. It was constructed using different materials, including stone, bricks, earth and wood. Watchtowers were built along the wall to help soldiers observe approaching enemies and communicate warnings.

In 1987, the Great Wall was added to the UNESCO World Heritage List. Today, it is considered a remarkable symbol of Chinese history, determination and engineering. Although some sections have been damaged, conservation projects help protect this extraordinary monument for future generations.

Skimming:
Read quickly to understand the general idea.
Scanning:
Look for dates, names, numbers and specific information.
MISSION 02 · TRAVELLING

Amine's Unforgettable Journey to London

Read the travel diary and discover what happened during the journey.

📖 Read the Text

Last summer, Amine, a fifteen-year-old Algerian pupil, travelled to London with his parents. It was his first trip abroad, so he was extremely excited. They arrived at Heathrow Airport on a sunny Saturday morning and took a train to their hotel.

On the first day, the family visited the famous Elizabeth Tower, which is commonly associated with the name Big Ben. Amine learnt that Big Ben is actually the nickname of the Great Bell inside the tower. He was amazed by the beautiful clock faces and the impressive Gothic architecture.

The following day, they went to Buckingham Palace. They watched the Changing of the Guard ceremony and took several photographs. Afterwards, they walked through St James's Park and enjoyed the peaceful atmosphere.

Finally, they visited the British Museum, where Amine discovered fascinating objects from different civilisations. He particularly enjoyed learning about ancient Egypt. Before returning to Algeria, he bought postcards for his classmates.

Amine described his holiday as an unforgettable experience. He realised that travelling is not only enjoyable but also educational because it helps people discover new cultures and understand the history of other countries.

🧭 Reading Strategy

Chronology:
Pay attention to time expressions such as "on the first day", "the following day" and "finally".
Inference:
Sometimes the answer is not directly stated. Use clues from the passage.
MISSION 03 · OUTSTANDING FIGURES

Lalla Fatma N'Soumer: A Courageous Algerian Woman

Complete this BEM-level reading comprehension mission.

📖 Read the Text

Lalla Fatma N'Soumer was an outstanding Algerian historical figure who became famous for her courage and resistance against French colonial rule. She was born in 1830 in the Kabylie region of Algeria.

From an early age, Fatma showed a strong personality, intelligence and determination. She was deeply interested in learning and religious studies. As she grew older, she became respected for her wisdom and leadership.

During the French conquest of Algeria, she supported the resistance movement in Kabylie. She encouraged people to defend their land and participated in organising resistance. Her determination inspired many people around her.

In 1857, she was captured by French forces. She was held in captivity until her death in 1863. Although her life was short, her courage left a lasting mark on Algerian history.

Today, Lalla Fatma N'Soumer is remembered as a symbol of bravery, patriotism and determination. Her story teaches younger generations the importance of courage, dignity and love for their country.
