MISSION 01 · FRIENDSHIP & PERSONALITY

My Best Childhood Friend

Read the passage and discover the qualities of a true friend.

📖 Read the Text

When I was a little child, I lived in a quiet neighbourhood in Sétif. My best friend was Yacine, a cheerful and generous boy who lived next door. We were the same age and attended the same primary school. Although we had different personalities, we became inseparable friends.

Yacine was tall and slim, with short curly black hair and bright brown eyes. He was extremely friendly and sociable. He enjoyed meeting new people and making everyone laugh. I was rather shy and quiet, but he always encouraged me to participate in activities. He was also honest and reliable. Whenever I needed help, he was there for me.

Every afternoon, we used to play football in the neighbourhood playground. Sometimes, we rode our bicycles or exchanged storybooks. During the summer holidays, we visited our grandparents and spent wonderful evenings telling stories. Those simple moments were the happiest memories of my childhood.

One day, Yacine's family moved to another city. I felt terribly sad because I thought our friendship would end. However, we continued communicating through phone calls and messages. Today, although we live far from each other, we are still close friends.

Yacine taught me that true friendship is not about living in the same place. It is about trust, kindness, respect and supporting each other in difficult moments.

🔎 Reading Strategy

Character description:
Look for adjectives describing appearance and personality.
Past habits:
Notice expressions such as "used to" and time markers.
MISSION 02 · CHILDHOOD EXPERIENCES

An Unforgettable Childhood Day

Read the story and follow the events in chronological order.

📖 Read the Text

When I was eleven years old, I experienced one of the most unforgettable days of my childhood. It happened during a school trip to a beautiful forest near our town. Our teacher organised the excursion to help us discover nature and learn about protecting the environment.

Early in the morning, we gathered in front of our school. Everyone was excited. After a short journey by bus, we arrived at the forest. The weather was sunny, the birds were singing, and the trees looked magnificent.

First, our teacher divided us into small groups. We explored the forest and identified different plants. Then, we collected litter to keep the area clean. Afterwards, we sat under a large tree and enjoyed our lunch together.

Suddenly, I noticed that my little backpack was missing. I became extremely worried because it contained my lunchbox, notebook and camera. My classmates immediately offered to help me. We searched everywhere until my friend Lina finally found it near a wooden bench. I felt incredibly relieved and thanked everyone.

At the end of the day, we returned home tired but happy. I learnt two important lessons: we must respect nature, and true friends always help each other. Even today, I remember that wonderful school trip with a big smile.

🧭 Reading Strategy

Chronological order:
Look for sequencers: first, then, afterwards, suddenly and finally.
Feelings:
Identify words describing emotions such as worried, relieved and happy.
MISSION 03 · DREAMS & AMBITIONS

From a Dream to Success

Complete this BEM-level reading comprehension mission.

📖 Read the Text

Since her early childhood, Amina had always dreamed of becoming a doctor. She grew up in a small Algerian village where medical services were limited. Whenever someone became ill, families sometimes had to travel long distances to reach a hospital. These experiences inspired her ambition.

Amina was a hardworking, patient and determined pupil. Although her family had limited financial resources, she never gave up. She studied regularly, borrowed books from the school library and asked her teachers for advice. Her favourite subjects were science and mathematics.

During her secondary school years, Amina faced several difficulties. She sometimes felt exhausted because she had to help her family and complete her homework. Nevertheless, she remained optimistic. Her teachers encouraged her, and her parents supported her dream.

After years of effort, Amina successfully passed her examinations and entered medical school. She worked extremely hard and eventually became a doctor. Later, she returned to her home region to help people who needed medical care.

Today, Amina is admired for her generosity and dedication. She believes that success requires patience, determination and continuous learning. Her story proves that difficult circumstances do not necessarily prevent people from achieving their dreams.

🎯 BEM Strategy

Main idea:
Identify what the whole passage is mainly about.
Inference:
Use information from different paragraphs to understand implied ideas.
Vocabulary:
Guess meanings using the surrounding words.
Pronoun reference:
Identify the person or thing replaced by a pronoun.
