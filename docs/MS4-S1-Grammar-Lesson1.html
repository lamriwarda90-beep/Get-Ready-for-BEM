👀 Discover the Passive Voice

Read these sentences. Pay attention to the highlighted parts.

🔍 Look closely at these examples

The Mona Lisa was painted by Leonardo da Vinci.
The pyramids were built thousands of years ago.
Subject Auxiliary Past Participle Agent
💡 What do you notice?

The sentences contain was or were followed by a past participle (V3).

The monument or work of art is placed first because it is the main focus of the sentence.

👉 This structure is called the Past Simple Passive.
02
Understand the Rule

📚 The Passive Voice Toolkit

We often use the passive when describing landmarks, inventions, books, works of art and historical achievements.

🌿 When do we use the passive?

We use the passive voice when we want to focus on the person, monument, object or achievement that receives the action.

SUBJECT + WAS / WERE + PAST PARTICIPLE (V3) + (BY + AGENT)

The person who performs the action is called the agent. We use by + agent when that information is important.

🟢 1. WAS

Use was with a singular subject.

SINGULAR + WAS + V3
The Eiffel Tower was designed by Gustave Eiffel.
Hamlet was written by Shakespeare.

🔵 2. WERE

Use were with a plural subject.

PLURAL + WERE + V3
The pyramids were built thousands of years ago.
Many monuments were restored.

🟠 3. Past Participle — V3

After was / were, always use the past participle.

WAS / WERE + V3
design → designed
paint → painted
write → written
build → built

🔴 4. BY + Agent

Use by when you want to mention who performed the action.

BY + AGENT
The Mona Lisa was painted by Leonardo da Vinci.
Hamlet was written by Shakespeare.
⚠️ Grammar Alert

After was / were, use V3.

❌ Hamlet was wrote by Shakespeare.
✅ Hamlet was written by Shakespeare.

❌ The pyramids were build long ago.
✅ The pyramids were built long ago.

📖 Useful Past Participles

Base Verb Past Simple Past Participle (V3)
design designed designed
paint painted painted
build built built
write wrote written
make made made
take took taken
give gave given
see saw seen
03
Active → Passive

🔄 How Does the Sentence Change?

Follow these three simple steps to transform an active sentence into a passive one.

ACTIVE VOICE
Gustave Eiffel designed the Eiffel Tower.
↓
PASSIVE VOICE
The Eiffel Tower was designed by Gustave Eiffel.
01 The active object becomes the passive subject.
02 The verb changes to was / were + V3.
03 The active subject can become by + agent.
🧠 Why use the passive?

Gustave Eiffel designed the Eiffel Tower.
→ Focus: Gustave Eiffel.

The Eiffel Tower was designed by Gustave Eiffel.
→ Focus: the Eiffel Tower.

This is why the passive is very useful when describing landmarks and achievements.
