This structure shows that two things have the same degree of a quality.

02
Understand the Rule

📚 Equality & Inequality Toolkit

We use these structures to compare people, places, landmarks and experiences.

🌿 The Main Rule

Use as ... as for equality and not as ... as for inequality.

AS + ADJECTIVE + AS
NOT AS + ADJECTIVE + AS

🟢 1. Equality

Use this structure when two things share the same degree of a quality.

A + be + as + adjective + as + B
Timgad is as fascinating as Djemila.
Trip A was as exciting as Trip B.

🔴 2. Inequality

Use this structure when the first thing has less of a quality than the second.

A + be + not as + adjective + as + B
The pyramid is not as tall as the skyscraper.
City B was not as quiet as City A.
Pyramids of Giza Egypt · Ancient monuments
Taj Mahal Agra, India · Historic monument
📖 الشرح بالعربية

نستعمل as + adjective + as عندما يكون شيئان متساويين في صفة معينة.

مثال:
Big Ben is as famous as the Eiffel Tower.

ونستعمل not as + adjective + as عندما يكون الشيء الأول أقل في الصفة من الشيء الثاني.

مثال:
The pyramid is not as tall as the skyscraper.
⚠️ Grammar Alert

The adjective does not change inside these structures.

✅ as beautiful as
❌ as more beautiful as

✅ not as tall as
❌ not as taller as
