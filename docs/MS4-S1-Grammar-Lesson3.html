02 LEARN THE RULES ⭐ EASY

📜 Superiority & Inferiority Rules

We use comparative adjectives to compare two people, places, objects, or landmarks.

🥇 Superiority

Superiority means that one thing has more of a quality than another.

SHORT ADJECTIVE
adjective + ER + THAN
The Burj Khalifa is taller than the Eiffel Tower.
The Great Pyramid is older than the Eiffel Tower.
LONG ADJECTIVE
MORE + adjective + THAN
The Burj Khalifa is more modern than the Great Pyramid.

🥈 Inferiority

Inferiority means that one thing has less of a quality than another.

LESS + adjective + THAN
The Eiffel Tower is less tall than the Burj Khalifa.
The Great Pyramid is less modern than the Eiffel Tower.

We can use less with both short and long adjectives.

📘 الشرح بالعربية:

المقارنة بالتفوق:
نستعملها عندما يكون الشيء الأول أكثر من الشيء الثاني في صفة معينة.
taller than = أطول من
older than = أقدم من
more famous than = أكثر شهرة من

المقارنة بالدونية:
نستعملها عندما يكون الشيء الأول أقل من الشيء الثاني في صفة معينة.
less tall than = أقل طولاً من
less modern than = أقل حداثة من

نستعمل THAN عند المقارنة بين شيئين.
💡 Remember:

Use -er with most short adjectives.
Use more with most longer adjectives.
Use less + adjective + than for inferiority.
03 GRAMMAR TOOLKIT ⭐⭐ LEARN

🧠 Comparative Adjective Toolkit

Study the spelling rules, irregular forms, and common mistakes.

✨ Regular Comparatives

Adjective Comparative Rule
tall taller Add -er
small smaller Add -er
large larger Add -r
big bigger Double the final consonant
heavy heavier Change y to i + er
famous more famous Use more
beautiful more beautiful Use more
interesting more interesting Use more

⚡ Irregular Comparatives

Adjective Comparative Example
good better This design is better than that one.
bad worse This road is worse than the other road.
far farther / further The museum is farther away than the theatre.

🚫 Common Mistakes

Incorrect ❌ Correct ✅
more taller taller
more bigger bigger
famouser more famous
less more beautiful less beautiful
better that better than
⚠️ تذكّر:

لا نستعمل MORE مع الصفة التي أضفنا إليها ER.
❌ more taller
✅ taller

بعض الصفات تتغير تماماً:
good → better
bad → worse

🪜 Your Writing Steps

1️⃣ Topic Sentence

Introduce the landmarks.

The Burj Khalifa, the Eiffel Tower, and the Great Pyramid are three famous landmarks.

2️⃣ Supporting Details

Compare their height, age, and other characteristics.

Use: taller than, older than, more modern than, less tall than.

3️⃣ Conclusion

End with your opinion.

All three landmarks are amazing and attract many visitors.

💡 Model paragraph:

The Burj Khalifa, the Eiffel Tower, and the Great Pyramid are three famous landmarks. The Burj Khalifa is taller than the Eiffel Tower. It is also more modern than the Great Pyramid. The Great Pyramid is older than both towers. However, it is less tall than the Burj Khalifa. All three landmarks are impressive and attract visitors from around the world.
