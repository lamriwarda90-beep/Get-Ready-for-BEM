02
Understand the Rule

📚 Chronological Markers Toolkit

Chronological markers are words and expressions that show the sequence of events.

🌿 The Main Rule

Use chronological markers to connect events and show what happened first, next and last.

FIRST → THEN → NEXT → AFTER THAT → LATER → FINALLY

They are especially useful in biographies, historical recounts and travel descriptions.

🥇 First

Introduces the first event.

First, we visited the museum.

➡️ Then

Introduces the following event.

Then, we walked to the monument.

➡️ Next

Introduces another event.

Next, we took photographs.

⏭️ After that

Shows what happened afterwards.

After that, we had lunch.

🕒 Later

Introduces an event at a later time.

Later, we visited the old city.

🏁 Finally

Introduces the last event.

Finally, we returned home.
📖 الشرح بالعربية

نستعمل أدوات الترتيب الزمني لتنظيم الأحداث حسب ترتيب وقوعها.

First = أولاً
Then = ثم
Next = بعد ذلك
After that = بعد ذلك
Later = لاحقاً
Finally = أخيراً

نستعمل هذه الأدوات عند سرد رحلة أو كتابة سيرة ذاتية أو وصف أحداث تاريخية.
⚠️ Grammar Alert

We often put a comma after a chronological marker at the beginning of a sentence.

✅ First, we visited the museum.
✅ After that, we took photographs.
✅ Finally, we returned home.

Remember: When recounting past events, we usually use the past simple tense.
