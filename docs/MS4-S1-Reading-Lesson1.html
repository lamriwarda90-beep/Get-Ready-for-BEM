STEP 02 · TEXT ONE

🕌 Read: The Great Mosque of Algiers

Read the text carefully. Discover an extraordinary Algerian landmark.

The Great Mosque of Algiers, also called Djamaa El Djazair, is one of Algeria's most impressive landmarks. It is located in Mohammadia, Algiers, near the Mediterranean Sea. The mosque is an important religious and cultural symbol of the country.

Construction of the mosque began in 2012. The main building work was completed in 2019, and the mosque was officially inaugurated in 2024. The complex combines modern engineering with traditional Algerian and Islamic architecture.

One of its most remarkable features is its enormous minaret, which is about 265 metres high. The complex also contains large prayer halls, a library, a museum, gardens and other facilities. Visitors can admire its spacious interiors and beautiful decorations.

Today, the Great Mosque of Algiers is more than a place of worship. It is a symbol of Algerian identity and heritage. Its magnificent architecture and location near the sea make it a remarkable landmark of the capital.

STEP 07 · TEXT TWO

🕰️ Read: Elizabeth Tower (Big Ben)

Travel to London and discover one of the most famous clock towers in the world.

Elizabeth Tower is one of the most famous landmarks in London, England. It stands at the north end of the Palace of Westminster, beside the River Thames. Many people call the tower Big Ben, but Big Ben is actually the nickname of the Great Bell inside it.

The tower was designed by Augustus Pugin as part of the new Palace of Westminster. It was completed in 1859. Originally called the Clock Tower, it was renamed Elizabeth Tower in 2012 to honour Queen Elizabeth II.

Elizabeth Tower is approximately 96 metres tall. It is built mainly of brick and covered with stone. The tower has four large clock faces, one on each side. Its Gothic Revival architecture makes it especially elegant and recognisable.

Today, Elizabeth Tower is an important symbol of London and the United Kingdom. Tourists from around the world visit Westminster to admire the tower, hear its famous bell and take photographs of this historic landmark.

📚 Remember: Elizabeth Tower is the tower. Big Ben is the nickname of its Great Bell.
