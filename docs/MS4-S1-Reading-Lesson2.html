Laarbi Ben M'hidi — A Symbol of Courage

Read the biography, then complete the comprehension tasks. Notice the dates, places, actions and qualities of the character.

Laarbi Ben M'hidi 🇩🇿

Laarbi Ben M'hidi was born in 1923 in Aïn M'lila, in eastern Algeria. He grew up in a period when Algeria was under French colonial rule. From a young age, he became interested in the situation of his country and believed that Algerians deserved freedom and dignity.

In the 1940s, Ben M'hidi joined the nationalist movement. He was an active member of the Organisation Spéciale and later became one of the important figures of the Algerian revolution. When the National Liberation Front (FLN) began the armed struggle in 1954, he played an important role in organising the movement.

During the Battle of Algiers in 1956, Ben M'hidi was responsible for important revolutionary activities in the capital. He was known for his courage, calmness and strong sense of responsibility. He encouraged people to remain united and to continue the struggle for independence.

Ben M'hidi was arrested by French forces in February 1957. Despite difficult circumstances, he remained courageous and refused to abandon his principles. He was killed in March 1957. After independence, he became a national symbol of sacrifice, patriotism and courage.

TEXT 2

William Shakespeare — The Bard of Avon

Now travel from revolutionary Algeria to Elizabethan England and discover the life of one of the world's best-known writers.

William Shakespeare 🎭
Hamlet — one of his famous plays
Romeo and Juliet

William Shakespeare was born in 1564 in Stratford-upon-Avon, England. He grew up in a family connected with local business and public life. As a young man, he developed an interest in language, stories and theatre.

Shakespeare later moved to London, where he became an actor and playwright. He worked with theatre companies and wrote plays for audiences from different social backgrounds. His works include tragedies, comedies and historical plays.

Among his most famous works are Hamlet, Romeo and Juliet and Macbeth. His plays often explore love, ambition, jealousy, power and human relationships. His language and memorable characters have influenced writers and artists for centuries.

Shakespeare died in 1616 in Stratford-upon-Avon. His works did not disappear after his death. They continued to be performed, studied and translated in many countries. Today, he is remembered as one of the most influential writers in English literature.
