TEXT 01

A Wonderful Journey Through Algiers

Read the travel story carefully. Pay attention to the places visited, landmarks described and historical figure mentioned.

The Casbah of Algiers 🇩🇿

Last spring, I travelled to Algiers, the capital of Algeria. I had always wanted to visit this historic city because it combines a rich history with beautiful Mediterranean scenery. When I arrived in the morning, the sea was shining and the streets were already full of life.

My first stop was the Casbah of Algiers, a historic neighbourhood with narrow streets, old houses and traditional buildings. As I walked through its lively streets, I imagined how people had lived there many years ago. The atmosphere was fascinating, and every corner seemed to tell a story.

Later, I visited the Martyr's Memorial. From there, I enjoyed a wonderful view of the city. The monument reminded me of the people who had sacrificed their lives for Algeria's independence. I also learned about important Algerian historical figures, including Emir Abdelkader, who became famous for his resistance and leadership in the nineteenth century.

In the afternoon, I continued my journey towards the coast and saw the impressive Great Mosque of Algiers. Its enormous minaret and modern architecture made a strong impression on me. Before leaving, I walked along the seafront and watched the sunset over the Mediterranean. It was a memorable day, and I promised myself to discover more of Algeria in the future.

TEXT 02

An Unforgettable Trip to Italy

Travel across Italy and discover ancient monuments, artistic treasures and famous cities.

The Colosseum — Rome 🇮🇹

During the summer holidays, my family and I travelled to Italy. It was my first visit to the country, so I was excited to discover its famous cities and landmarks. Our journey began in Rome, a city where ancient history can be seen almost everywhere.

On our first morning, we visited the impressive Colosseum. As I stood in front of the huge ancient building, I imagined the crowds who had watched shows there many centuries ago. We then walked through the historic centre and admired several old buildings and beautiful squares.

After Rome, we travelled to Florence, a city famous for art and culture. In a museum, I discovered the work of Leonardo da Vinci, one of the great artists and thinkers of the Renaissance. I was fascinated by his creativity and by the importance of Florence in the history of art.

Our final destination was Venice. Instead of ordinary streets, the city seemed to be crossed by water. We travelled along its canals and admired the beautiful buildings. The atmosphere was peaceful and magical. On the way home, I realised that Italy was much more than a holiday destination: it was a journey through history, art and culture. I returned home with wonderful memories and a strong desire to visit Italy again.
