02 · LEARN

📍 Location & Description Toolkit

Use these useful expressions to describe famous places.

🧭 Simple location

is located in / is situated in + city, country

“The Sagrada Família is located in Barcelona, Spain.”

🏞️ Descriptive location

overlooks / lies in the heart of / is perched on a hill above

“The Casbah overlooks the Bay of Algiers.”

🏺 Age & history

ancient / dates back to / was built in

“The Alhambra dates back to the 13th century.”

✨ Grandeur

iconic / majestic / towering / breathtaking

“The Colosseum is an iconic amphitheatre.”

🎨 Craftsmanship

renowned for / famous for / intricate architecture

“Topkapı Palace is famous for its intricate tile-work.”

🌍 Heritage

historic site / UNESCO World Heritage Site

“The Casbah is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.”

⚠️ Historic vs Historical

Historic means important in history. Historical means related to history.

Example: The Casbah is a historic site. It contains many historical buildings.

The Alhambra Palace is located in Granada, in the south of Spain. This majestic palace and fortress is perched on a hill above the city, overlooking the Sierra Nevada mountains.
