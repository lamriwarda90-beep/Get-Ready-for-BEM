03 · ORGANISE

🧩 The Biography Journey

Follow five stages to organise a clear biography.

👶 1. Birth was born in ... in ...
🎓 2. Education studied / learned ...
💼 3. Career became / worked as ...
🏆 4. Achievements created / wrote / led ...
🌍 5. Legacy is remembered for ...
Example: Vincent van Gogh was born in the Netherlands in 1853. He became a painter. He created Starry Night. Today, he is remembered for his expressive paintings.
