06 · READ

📖 A Day in London

Read the trip report carefully. Then answer the comprehension questions.

Last Saturday, my classmates and I travelled to London. We took the train and arrived in the city in the morning.

First, we visited Big Ben and took lots of photographs. Then, we walked along the River Thames and crossed Westminster Bridge.

At lunchtime, we ate at a small restaurant. The food was delicious.

In the afternoon, we visited the London Eye and enjoyed the beautiful view of the city.

Finally, we returned to the station and caught our train home. It was an unforgettable trip!
