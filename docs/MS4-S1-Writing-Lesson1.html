Become a Landmark Writer! ✨

Follow six small steps. You do not need to write the whole paragraph at once.

01

Understand Your Mission 🎯

Situation of Integration — BEM-style Writing

Your English club is preparing a travel guide called “Discover the World's Amazing Landmarks”. You want to introduce a famous place to foreign visitors.

Write one organised paragraph of about 8–10 sentences describing the Leaning Tower of Pisa using the fact file.

Mention its name, location, construction dates, height, weight, material, appearance and importance.

اكتب فقرة وصفية من 8 إلى 10 جمل حول برج بيزا المائل باستعمال بطاقة المعلومات. اذكر الموقع وتاريخ البناء والارتفاع والوزن والمواد والشكل والأهمية.

02

Discover a Model Paragraph 🔎

Read about the Eiffel Tower. Tap any coloured sentence to discover its role.

The Eiffel Tower is one of the most famous landmarks in the world. It is located in Paris, the capital city of France. It was built between 1887 and 1889 for the World's Fair. The tower is about 330 metres high and weighs approximately 10,100 tonnes. Moreover, it is made mainly of iron and consists of four large legs joined by a remarkable metal structure. Its unusual shape makes it easy to recognise from a distance. In addition, millions of visitors come to admire it every year. For these reasons, the Eiffel Tower remains a symbol of Paris and an extraordinary achievement in architecture.
