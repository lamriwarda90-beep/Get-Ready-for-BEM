🎨 Discover Pablo Picasso

Read this model biography carefully. Notice how the writer introduces the person, presents life events in order and finishes with a concluding sentence.

Pablo Picasso
Pablo Picasso was a famous Spanish artist who changed modern art. He was born in Málaga, Spain, in 1881 and began drawing and painting at an early age. Later, he moved to Barcelona and continued his artistic studies. In 1904, he settled in Paris, where he developed several important artistic styles, including Cubism. Moreover, he produced paintings, drawings, sculptures, prints and ceramics. One of his most famous paintings is Guernica, created in 1937. Finally, Picasso remained one of the most influential artists of the twentieth century until his death in 1973.
🔵 Topic sentence 🟡 Life details 🟢 Linking words 🟣 Conclusion

🔎 Understand the Biography

Choose the correct function for each part.

1. “Pablo Picasso was a famous Spanish artist...”
2. “He was born in Málaga in 1881.”
3. “Later, he moved to Barcelona...”
4. “Picasso remained one of the most influential artists...”
💡 Remember: A biography usually contains a topic sentence, personal information, education, achievements, important events and a conclusion.

Malek Bennabi

🇩🇿 An Outstanding Algerian Thinker

Malek Bennabi was an Algerian thinker, writer and philosopher.

He is remembered for his important ideas about culture, civilisation and society.

مالك بن نبي مفكر وكاتب وفيلسوف جزائري، اشتهر بأفكاره حول الثقافة والحضارة والمجتمع.
Full name Malek Bennabi
Date of birth 1 January 1905
Place of birth Constantine, Algeria
Profession Thinker, writer and philosopher
Education Studied in Algeria and France
Main interests Culture, civilisation and society
Important books The Conditions of the Renaissance; The Quranic Phenomenon
Major contribution Developed important ideas about civilisation and culture
Returned to Algeria 1963
Date and place of death 31 October 1973, Algiers
