01
SITUATION OF INTEGRATION

📮 Your Travel Writing Mission

Last summer, you visited London with your family. You saw wonderful landmarks, took photographs and enjoyed your holiday.

Your English teacher is preparing a class magazine called “Our Amazing Journeys” and asks you to write about your trip.

Write a paragraph of 80–100 words about your past trip. Mention when and where you went, who accompanied you, the landmarks you visited, what you did and how you felt. Use the past simple and chronological linking words.

المهمة: اكتب فقرة من 80 إلى 100 كلمة عن رحلة سابقة إلى لندن. اذكر متى سافرت، ومع من، والمعالم التي زرتها، وما فعلته، وكيف شعرت. استعمل الماضي البسيط وأدوات الترتيب الزمني.

02
READ · NOTICE · UNDERSTAND

📖 Read a Model Travel Report

Read the paragraph. The colours show the opening, the details, the chronological linking words and the ending.

Last summer, I travelled to London with my family for five wonderful days. First, we visited Big Ben, the famous clock tower near the Houses of Parliament. We took beautiful photos beside the River Thames. Next, we went to Tower Bridge and walked across it. The view was amazing! Then, we rode the London Eye and saw the city from above. After that, we visited Buckingham Palace and watched the guards. Finally, we returned home feeling happy and excited. It was an unforgettable trip!
🔵 Opening 🟡 Events and descriptions 🟢 Sequencers 🟣 Ending and feelings

🧠 Practise Past Simple and Sequencers

1. Yesterday, we ___ Big Ben.
2. We ___ many beautiful photographs.
3. We ___ the city from the London Eye.

تذكّر: نستعمل الماضي البسيط للحديث عن أحداث انتهت. الأفعال غير المنتظمة: went = ذهب، saw = رأى، took = التقط. أدوات الترتيب: First = أولاً، Next = بعد ذلك، Then = ثم، Finally = أخيراً.

05
WRITE · EDIT · IMPROVE

✍️ Your Writing Studio

Read your draft. Improve it by adding details about the landmarks, your activities and your feelings. Aim for 80–100 words.

🌟 Writer's tip: Use the past simple to narrate events. Use descriptive adjectives and qualifiers to describe landmarks: very beautiful, extremely tall, really impressive . Use First, Next, Then, After that, Finally to connect events.
