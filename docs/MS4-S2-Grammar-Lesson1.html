1. Present Simple

HABITS · ROUTINES · PERSONALITY · FACTS

🎯 When do we use the Present Simple?

We use the Present Simple to describe:

  • Daily habits:
    I usually read books after school.
  • Routines:
    My sister studies English every evening.
  • Personality traits:
    My best friend always helps people.
  • Preferences:
    I enjoy reading biographies.
  • Permanent situations:
    I live with my family in Algeria.

🧱 The Three Forms

✅ Affirmative

Subject + Verb

I help my friends.

She helps her friends.

Remember: He / She / It + verb-s

❌ Negative

Subject + don't / doesn't + Verb

I don't like violence.

She doesn't give up easily.

After doesn't, use the base verb.

❓ Interrogative

Do / Does + Subject + Verb?

Do you enjoy reading?

Does she help others?

Yes, she does.
No, she doesn't.

⚠️ Spelling Rules

Most verbs take -s:
help → helps
read → reads

Verbs ending in -ch, -sh, -s, -x or -o usually take -es:
watch → watches
go → goes

Consonant + y → -ies:
study → studies
try → tries

Vowel + y → -s:
play → plays

Special verb:
have → has

🚫 Common Mistakes

❌ She don't like reading.
✅ She doesn't like reading.

❌ My brother study hard.
✅ My brother studies hard.

❌ Does she likes English?
✅ Does she like English?

⏰ Time Expressions

always usually often sometimes never every day every weekend

شرح المضارع البسيط

نستعمل المضارع البسيط للتعبير عن العادات اليومية والروتين والصفات الشخصية والحقائق.

مثال:
I usually help my mother.
أنا عادة أساعد أمي.

في الجملة المثبتة نضيف s أو es إلى الفعل مع He و She و It.

في النفي نستعمل don't أو doesn't ثم الفعل في صورته الأصلية.

في السؤال نبدأ بـ Do أو Does.

انتبه: بعد Does و Doesn't لا نضيف s إلى الفعل.

2. Past Simple

CHILDHOOD · MEMORIES · LIFE EXPERIENCES

🎯 When do we use the Past Simple?

We use the Past Simple for actions or situations that happened and finished in the past.

  • Childhood memories:
    When I was six, I learned to ride a bicycle.
  • Life experiences:
    I visited a science exhibition last year.
  • Achievements:
    My sister won a school competition.
  • Past situations:
    I was very shy when I was young.

🧱 The Three Forms

✅ Affirmative

Subject + Past Verb

I visited my uncle.

She won a prize.

Use the past form of the verb.

❌ Negative

Subject + didn't + Base Verb

I didn't visit London.

She didn't win the prize.

After didn't, use the base verb.

❓ Interrogative

Did + Subject + Base Verb?

Did you visit Algiers?

Did she win a prize?

Yes, she did.
No, she didn't.

📌 Regular Verbs

Most regular verbs take -ed.

visit → visited
help → helped
work → worked
start → started

Verbs ending in consonant + y:

study → studied
try → tried

Verbs ending in -e take -d:

live → lived

📌 Irregular Verbs

Some verbs have special past forms.

go → went
see → saw
meet → met
write → wrote
become → became
win → won
have → had
be → was / were

⭐ Special Verb: BE

I / He / She / It → was
You / We / They → were

Affirmative: I was shy.
Negative: I wasn't shy.
Question: Were you shy?

Do not use did with the past forms of BE.

🚫 Common Mistakes

❌ Did you visited your grandparents?
✅ Did you visit your grandparents?

❌ I didn't went to school.
✅ I didn't go to school.

❌ She was very shy? (Yes/No question)
✅ Was she very shy?

⏰ Time Expressions

yesterday last year last weekend two years ago when I was young in 2020

شرح الماضي البسيط

نستعمل الماضي البسيط للتعبير عن أحداث وقعت وانتهت في الماضي.

مثال:
I visited my grandparents yesterday.
زرت جدي وجدتي أمس.

في الإثبات نستعمل الفعل في الماضي. الأفعال المنتظمة تأخذ ed، أما الأفعال الشاذة فلها تصريف خاص.

في النفي نستعمل didn't مع الفعل الأصلي.

في السؤال نستعمل Did ثم الفاعل ثم الفعل الأصلي.

الفعل be في الماضي هو was أو were، ونستعمله دون did.

⏳

3. Past Continuous

ACTIONS IN PROGRESS · PAST MEMORIES

🎯 When do we use the Past Continuous?

We use the Past Continuous to describe an action that was happening at a particular moment in the past.

  • Action in progress:
    I was reading a biography at 8 p.m.
  • Interrupted action:
    I was studying when my friend called.
  • Two simultaneous actions:
    While I was reading, my sister was drawing.

🧱 The Three Forms

✅ Affirmative

Subject + was/were + Verb-ing

I was reading.

They were studying.

❌ Negative

Subject + wasn't/weren't + Verb-ing

I wasn't sleeping.

They weren't playing.

❓ Interrogative

Was/Were + Subject + Verb-ing?

Were you studying?

Was she reading?

⭐ WAS or WERE?

I / He / She / It → was
You / We / They → were

🧠 Past Continuous + Past Simple

We often use the Past Continuous for the longer action and the Past Simple for the shorter action that interrupts it.

I was preparing my project when the teacher called.

Long action: was preparing
Short action: called

✍️ Spelling Rules for -ING

read → reading
study → studying
write → writing
make → making
run → running
sit → sitting

🚫 Common Mistakes

❌ I was study English.
✅ I was studying English.

❌ They was playing.
✅ They were playing.

❌ I were reading.
✅ I was reading.

⏰ Time Expressions

while at that moment at 8 p.m. yesterday when

شرح الماضي المستمر

نستعمل الماضي المستمر للتعبير عن حدث كان مستمراً في وقت معين في الماضي.

الصيغة:
was / were + verb-ing

مثال:
I was reading at 8 p.m.
كنت أقرأ على الساعة الثامنة مساءً.

نستعمل was مع I و He و She و It، ونستعمل were مع You و We و They.

يمكن أن نستعمل الماضي المستمر مع الماضي البسيط للتعبير عن حدث قصير قاطع حدثاً مستمراً.

4. Future with WILL

DREAMS · AMBITIONS · PROMISES · PREDICTIONS

🎯 When do we use WILL?

We use WILL to talk about:

  • Future ambitions:
    One day, I will become an engineer.
  • Predictions:
    I think my sister will be successful.
  • Promises:
    I will always help my parents.
  • Instant decisions:
    I will help you with your homework.

🧱 The Three Forms

✅ Affirmative

Subject + will + Base Verb

I will study harder.

She will become a doctor.

❌ Negative

Subject + will not + Base Verb

I will not give up.

She won't fail.

❓ Interrogative

Will + Subject + Base Verb?

Will you work hard?

Will she become a doctor?

Yes, she will.
No, she won't.

⭐ Important Rules

WILL is the same with all subjects.

I will
You will
He will
She will
We will
They will

The verb after WILL is always in its base form.

will not = won't

🚫 Common Mistakes

❌ I will to become a scientist.
✅ I will become a scientist.

❌ She will studies harder.
✅ She will study harder.

❌ He wills become a doctor.
✅ He will become a doctor.

⏰ Time Expressions

tomorrow next year next week one day in the future

شرح المستقبل باستعمال WILL

نستعمل will للتعبير عن التوقعات والوعود والقرارات اللحظية والطموحات المستقبلية.

مثال:
I will become a doctor.
سأصبح طبيباً.

الإثبات:
Subject + will + verb

النفي:
Subject + won't + verb

السؤال:
Will + subject + verb?

مهم: بعد will نستعمل الفعل الأصلي دون to ودون s.
