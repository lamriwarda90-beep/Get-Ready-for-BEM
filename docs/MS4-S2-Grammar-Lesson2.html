DISCOVER · UNDERSTAND

📚 Two Tenses, Two Stories

Before using WHEN and WHILE, learn the difference between the Past Simple and the Past Continuous.

⚡

Past Simple

We use the Past Simple for actions that happened and finished in the past.

  • Completed past actions
  • Childhood memories
  • Past achievements
  • Short actions that interrupt longer actions
Subject + Past Verb
I won a competition last year.
My teacher called me yesterday.
I met my best friend in primary school.

Negative

Subject + didn't + Base Verb
I didn't forget my childhood friend.

Question

Did + Subject + Base Verb?
Did you enjoy your childhood?
⏳

Past Continuous

We use the Past Continuous for actions that were happening at a particular moment in the past.

  • Actions in progress in the past
  • Background actions in stories
  • Longer actions interrupted by short events
  • Two actions happening at the same time
Subject + was/were + Verb-ing
I was reading my favourite biography.
My friends were preparing a school project.

Negative

Subject + wasn't/weren't + Verb-ing
I wasn't sleeping at 8 p.m.

Question

Was/Were + Subject + Verb-ing?
Were you studying yesterday evening?

🧠 Remember WAS and WERE

Subject Auxiliary Example
I / He / She / It was She was reading.
You / We / They were They were studying.

📊 Understanding the Two Timelines

⚡ Pattern A: Interrupted Action

A longer action was happening when a shorter event interrupted it.

Long action Short event ⚡
I was writing my life story when my friend called.

was writing = long action
called = short action

🔄 Pattern B: Simultaneous Actions

Two longer actions were happening at the same time.

While I was studying, my sister was drawing.

Both actions were in progress at the same time.

🌸 WHEN vs WHILE

Connector Common Pattern Example
WHEN Past Continuous + when + Past Simple I was reading when the phone rang.
WHILE While + Past Continuous, Past Continuous While I was reading, my brother was studying.
WHILE While + Past Continuous, Past Simple While I was walking, I met an old friend.

💡 Important!

WHEN and WHILE are not restricted to only one tense. What matters is the meaning: whether an action was completed or was in progress.

I was studying when my mother arrived.
While I was studying, my mother arrived.

Both sentences are grammatically correct.

شرح الماضي البسيط والماضي المستمر

الماضي البسيط: نستعمله للتعبير عن حدث وقع وانتهى في الماضي.

مثال:
I visited my grandmother yesterday.
زرت جدتي أمس.

الماضي المستمر: نستعمله للتعبير عن حدث كان مستمراً في وقت معين في الماضي.

الصيغة:
was / were + verb-ing

مثال:
I was reading when my friend called.
كنت أقرأ عندما اتصل بي صديقي.

WHEN: تُستعمل غالباً مع الحدث القصير الذي وقع أثناء حدث مستمر.

WHILE: تُستعمل غالباً مع حدث كان مستمراً، ويمكن أن تربط بين حدثين مستمرين في الوقت نفسه.

02
MASTER THE DETAILS

⚠️ Important Rules & Tricky Cases

Study these rules to avoid common grammar mistakes.

1️⃣ Stative Verbs

Some verbs describe states, feelings or thoughts. They are not normally used in continuous forms.

Examples: know, believe, understand, want, need, love, hate

✅ I knew my best friend when I was young.
❌ I was knowing my best friend.

Some stative verbs can have continuous forms when their meaning changes, but beginners should first master their usual stative meanings.

2️⃣ The Comma Rule

When a WHEN or WHILE clause comes first, we usually put a comma after it.

While I was studying, my sister was sleeping.

When the main clause comes first, we normally do not need a comma.

My sister was sleeping while I was studying.

3️⃣ Spelling Rules for -ING

Rule Examples
Most verbs: add -ing read → reading
Final silent -e: remove e write → writing
Short stressed vowel + consonant: double consonant run → running
Final -ie: change to -ying lie → lying

4️⃣ Past Simple Irregular Verbs

Some verbs do not take -ed.

Base Verb Past Simple
seesaw
meetmet
writewrote
gowent
knowknew
winwon

5️⃣ Common Mistakes

❌ I was study when she arrived.
✅ I was studying when she arrived.
❌ They was playing.
✅ They were playing.
❌ I didn't went to school.
✅ I didn't go to school.
❌ While I was reading I was listening to music.
✅ While I was reading, I was listening to music.

قواعد مهمة

بعض الأفعال مثل know و want و believe تعبّر عادة عن حالات وليست أفعالاً مستمرة، لذلك لا نستعملها غالباً بصيغة ing.

إذا بدأت الجملة بـ When أو While، نضع عادة فاصلة بعد الجملة التابعة.

عند إضافة ing نحذف حرف e الأخير في أفعال مثل write → writing.

بعض الأفعال شاذة في الماضي مثل: go → went و see → saw.
