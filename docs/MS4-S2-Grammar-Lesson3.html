🧠 Understanding the Superlative

We use the superlative degree to compare three or more people, things, or experiences and identify the one with the highest or lowest degree of a quality.

Example:
Sara is tall.
Lina is taller than Sara.
Amira is the tallest girl in the class.

🌸 Rule 1: Short Adjectives

Most adjectives with one syllable take -est.

the + adjective + est
young → the youngest
tall → the tallest
small → the smallest

My grandfather is the oldest person in our family.

نستعمل صيغة التفضيل العليا للمقارنة بين ثلاثة أشخاص أو أشياء أو أكثر. مع الصفات القصيرة نضيف est إلى الصفة ونضع the قبلها.

💜 Rule 2: Long Adjectives

Most longer adjectives take the most before the adjective.

the most + adjective
beautiful → the most beautiful
intelligent → the most intelligent
exciting → the most exciting

Winning the competition was the most exciting experience of my life.

مع معظم الصفات الطويلة لا نضيف est. بل نستعمل the most قبل الصفة. مثال: the most interesting = الأكثر تشويقاً.

⭐ Rule 3: Irregular Adjectives

Some adjectives have special superlative forms. We must memorise them.

good → the best
bad → the worst
far → the farthest / furthest
little → the least
much / many → the most

My childhood was the best period of my life.

توجد صفات شاذة لا تتبع القواعد السابقة. مثال: good تصبح best، و bad تصبح worst. يجب حفظ هذه الصيغ.

📍 Rule 4: IN and OF

We often use in with places and groups such as a class or family.

She is the youngest pupil in our class.

We use of with expressions such as all, the three, and my life.

This is the happiest day of my life.
نستعمل in مع الأماكن أو المجموعات مثل القسم والعائلة. ونستعمل of مع تعابير مثل of all و of my life.

📊 Comparative vs Superlative

Adjective Comparative Superlative
young younger the youngest
happy happier the happiest
beautiful more beautiful the most beautiful
good better the best
bad worse the worst

⚠️ Spelling Rules and Exceptions

Pay attention to these spelling changes. They will help you avoid common mistakes.

1. Adjectives ending in -e
Add only -st.
nice → nicest
large → largest
2. Consonant + y
Change y to i and add -est.
happy → happiest
funny → funniest
easy → easiest
3. Consonant–vowel–consonant
For certain short adjectives, double the final consonant.
big → biggest
hot → hottest
thin → thinnest
4. Some two-syllable adjectives
Certain adjectives ending in -y, -ow, -er or -le can take -est.
narrow → narrowest
clever → cleverest
simple → simplest
5. Avoid double superlatives
❌ the most tallest
✅ the tallest

❌ the most happiest
✅ the happiest
قواعد مهمة:
1. إذا انتهت الصفة بحرف e نضيف st فقط.
2. إذا انتهت الصفة بحرف ساكن ثم y نحول y إلى i ونضيف est.
3. في بعض الصفات القصيرة نضاعف الحرف الأخير.
4. لا نستعمل most و est معاً.
