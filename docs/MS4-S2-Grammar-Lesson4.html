LEARN · 02

🧠 Understanding the Grammar Rules

Each comparison marker has a different meaning and grammatical structure.

🪞 Rule 1: LIKE

We use like to show that two people or things are similar.

Like + noun/pronoun, + subject + verb
Like her mother, Sara is very patient.

Like my brother, I enjoy playing football.

We can also put like after the verb.

Subject + verb + like + noun/pronoun
She sings like her mother.

He behaves like his father.
نستعمل like للتعبير عن التشابه بين شخصين أو شيئين.
معناها: مثل أو يشبه.

مثال:
Like her mother, Sara is patient.
سارة صبورة مثل أمها.

بعد like نستعمل عادة اسماً أو ضميراً.

🔄 Rule 2: UNLIKE

We use unlike to show that two people or things are different.

Unlike + noun/pronoun, + subject + verb
Unlike his brother, Amir is very shy.

Unlike my sister, I enjoy reading.

Unlike can also appear after a verb.

Subject + verb + unlike + noun/pronoun
I prefer quiet places, unlike my friends.
نستعمل unlike للتعبير عن الاختلاف.
معناها: على عكس أو بخلاف.

مثال:
Unlike her sister, Lina is shy.
على عكس أختها، لينا خجولة.

إذا بدأنا الجملة بـ unlike نضع فاصلة بعد الاسم أو الضمير الذي يليها.

⚖️ Rule 3: WHEREAS

We use whereas to connect two contrasting ideas.

Clause 1, whereas + Clause 2
Sara is outgoing, whereas Lina is shy.

I love reading, whereas my brother prefers sports.

A clause normally contains a subject and a verb.

Whereas + Clause 1, + Clause 2
Whereas Sara is outgoing, Lina is shy.
نستعمل whereas للربط بين فكرتين متناقضتين أو مختلفتين.
معناها: بينما أو في حين أن.

مثال:
Sara is active, whereas Lina is quiet.
سارة نشيطة، بينما لينا هادئة.

بعد whereas نستعمل جملة تحتوي عادة على فاعل وفعل.

🎭 Rule 4: LIKE vs AS

Do not confuse like with as.

Like means "similar to".

He works like a robot.
He works in a way that resembles a robot.

As can describe a person's real job or role.

He works as a teacher.
Teaching is his actual profession.
نستعمل like للتشبيه.
He works like a robot.
يعمل مثل الروبوت.

أما as فنستعملها هنا للتعبير عن وظيفة أو دور حقيقي.
He works as a teacher.
يعمل أستاذاً.

⚠️ Important Rules & Common Mistakes

These small details will help you write more accurate comparison sentences.

1. Use a comma after an opening LIKE phrase.

✓ Like her mother, Sara is patient.
✗ Like her mother Sara is patient.
2. Use a comma after an opening UNLIKE phrase.

✓ Unlike his brother, Amir is shy.
✗ Unlike his brother Amir is shy.
3. WHEREAS connects two clauses.

✓ Sara is confident, whereas Lina is shy.
✗ Sara is confident, whereas Lina.

The second part needs a verb.
4. LIKE and ALIKE are different.

✓ The twins look alike.
✓ Sara looks like her sister.
✗ The twins look like.
5. Do not confuse LIKE and UNLIKE.

LIKE = similarity.
UNLIKE = difference.

Example:
Like her sister, Sara is friendly.
Unlike her sister, Sara is shy.
ملخص القواعد المهمة:

1. نستعمل like للتشابه.
2. نستعمل unlike للاختلاف.
3. نستعمل whereas للربط بين جملتين متناقضتين.
4. بعد like و unlike يأتي غالباً اسم أو ضمير.
5. بعد whereas تأتي جملة تحتوي على فاعل وفعل.
6. انتبه إلى استعمال الفاصلة في المكان الصحيح.
