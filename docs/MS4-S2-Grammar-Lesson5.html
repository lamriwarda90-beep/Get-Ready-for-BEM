Discover the Rule!
We can add a small group of letters at the beginning of a word to change its meaning.

This group of letters is called a PREFIX.
السابقة (Prefix) هي حرف أو مجموعة حروف نضيفها في بداية الكلمة لتغيير معناها.

مثال:
happy = سعيد
unhappy = غير سعيد

patient = صبور
impatient = غير صبور

تسمى هذه السوابق سوابق النفي لأنها تُكوّن كلمات تحمل معنى النفي أو العكس.
LEARN · 02

🧠 The Six Negative Prefixes

Negative prefixes are added to the beginning of certain words to form their negative or opposite meanings.

General Structure:

PREFIX + BASE WORD = NEW WORD

Example:
un + happy = unhappy

🌸 1. Prefix UN-

The prefix un- is commonly used to form the opposite of many adjectives.

un + adjective
happy → unhappy
kind → unkind
fair → unfair
friendly → unfriendly
lucky → unlucky

Sequence 2 example:

Amir felt unhappy when he failed his first test.

An unkind person may hurt other people's feelings.
نستعمل السابقة un- مع عدد كبير من الصفات لتكوين المعنى العكسي.

happy = سعيد
unhappy = غير سعيد

kind = لطيف
unkind = غير لطيف

fair = عادل
unfair = غير عادل

💜 2. Prefix IN-

The prefix in- forms the opposite of certain adjectives.

in + adjective
active → inactive
correct → incorrect
complete → incomplete
dependent → independent
secure → insecure

Sequence 2 example:

When I was younger, I was insecure about speaking in public.

Sara is becoming more independent as she grows older.
نستعمل السابقة in- مع بعض الصفات لتكوين معنى النفي أو العكس.

active = نشيط
inactive = غير نشيط

correct = صحيح
incorrect = غير صحيح

secure = واثق / آمن
insecure = غير واثق / غير آمن

🌟 3. Prefix DIS-

The prefix dis- can form negative adjectives and opposite verbs.

dis + adjective / verb
honest → dishonest
loyal → disloyal
agree → disagree
like → dislike
obey → disobey

Sequence 2 example:

A dishonest person does not always tell the truth.

I dislike getting up very early.
نستعمل السابقة dis- مع بعض الصفات والأفعال لتكوين معنى معاكس.

honest = صادق
dishonest = غير صادق

agree = يوافق
disagree = لا يوافق / يختلف

like = يحب
dislike = لا يحب

💙 4. Prefix IL-

The prefix il- is used with certain adjectives beginning with the letter L.

il + adjective beginning with L
legal → illegal
logical → illogical
legible → illegible

Sequence 2 example:

Sara thinks cheating is an illegal act in some circumstances.

His explanation was illogical.
نستعمل السابقة il- مع بعض الصفات التي تبدأ بالحرف L.

legal = قانوني
illegal = غير قانوني

logical = منطقي
illogical = غير منطقي

ملاحظة:
ليست كل كلمة تبدأ بالحرف L تأخذ السابقة il-.

🔮 5. Prefix IR-

The prefix ir- is used with certain adjectives beginning with the letter R.

ir + adjective beginning with R
regular → irregular
responsible → irresponsible
relevant → irrelevant
rational → irrational

Sequence 2 example:

Amir was irresponsible when he forgot his homework.

My childhood routine was sometimes irregular.
نستعمل السابقة ir- مع بعض الصفات التي تبدأ بالحرف R.

regular = منتظم
irregular = غير منتظم

responsible = مسؤول
irresponsible = غير مسؤول

🍀 6. Prefix IM-

The prefix im- is used with certain adjectives beginning with M or P.

It is also found in some words beginning with B.

im + selected adjectives
patient → impatient
polite → impolite
possible → impossible
mature → immature
balanced → imbalanced

Sequence 2 example:

When I was a child, I was very impatient.

Now I try not to be impolite when I disagree with someone.
نستعمل السابقة im- مع بعض الصفات التي تبدأ بالحرفين M أو P، وتوجد أيضاً مع بعض الكلمات التي تبدأ بـ B.

patient = صبور
impatient = غير صبور

polite = مؤدب
impolite = غير مؤدب

possible = ممكن
impossible = مستحيل

🌳 The IN- Prefix Family

One Prefix Family — Different Forms

The prefixes in-, im-, il-, and ir- are related forms of the same negative prefix. The spelling changes in certain words.

in-
inactive, incorrect, incomplete
Used with selected adjectives.
im-
impatient, immature, imbalanced
Often appears before P, M, and B.
il-
illegal, illogical, illegible
Appears before L in certain words.
ir-
irregular, irresponsible, irrelevant
Appears before R in certain words.
Very Important!
The first letter can help you choose between in-, im-, il-, and ir-, but it does not tell you whether a word takes this prefix family.

For example:
unhappy is correct.
inhappy is incorrect.

You must learn which prefix belongs to each word.

📊 Quick Reference Table

Prefix Base Word Negative Word Meaning
un- happy unhappy not happy
un- kind unkind not kind
in- active inactive not active
in- correct incorrect not correct
dis- honest dishonest not honest
dis- agree disagree not agree
il- legal illegal not legal
ir- responsible irresponsible not responsible
im- patient impatient not patient
im- possible impossible not possible
LEARN · 03

⚠️ Important Rules & Common Mistakes

Be careful! We cannot add any negative prefix to any word.

1. Do not invent negative words.

✓ unhappy
✗ inhappy

✓ dishonest
✗ unhonest
2. Remember the spelling of the IN- family.

✓ impossible
✗ inpossible

✓ illegal
✗ inlegal

✓ irregular
✗ inregular
3. A prefix does not always make a word negative.

Not every word beginning with in-, im-, or dis- contains a negative prefix.

For example:
important does not mean "not portant".
discover does not mean "not cover".
4. Some words have special meanings.

independent means able to act or live without relying on other people.

disappointed means unhappy because something did not happen as expected.

Knowing the meaning of the whole word is important.
5. Negative prefixes are not the same as NOT.

Compare:
Sara is not happy.
Sara is unhappy.

Both sentences express a similar meaning.

However, not every adjective has a commonly used negative-prefix form.
قواعد مهمة:

1. لا يمكن إضافة أي سابقة إلى أي كلمة.

2. يجب حفظ الكلمة مع السابقة المناسبة لها.

3. توجد علاقة بين السوابق in- و im- و il- و ir-.

4. يتغير شكل السابقة أحياناً حسب الحرف الذي تبدأ به الكلمة.

5. ليست كل كلمة تبدأ بهذه الحروف كلمة منفية.

MISSION 07 ⭐⭐⭐ HARD

✍️ My Personality Journey

Write a short paragraph about your personality when you were younger and how you have changed. Use at least three negative-prefix words.

Writing Guide:

1. Begin with your childhood personality.
2. Describe one or two negative traits you had in the past.
3. Explain how you have changed.
4. Finish with a positive sentence about your personality today.

Example: When I was a child, I was sometimes impatient and unhappy.

Example: I sometimes behaved irresponsibly, but I learned from my mistakes.

Example: Today, I try not to be impolite or dishonest.
