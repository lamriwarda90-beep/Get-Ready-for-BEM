Discover the Rule!

The letters -ful and -less are added to the end of certain words.

They are called SUFFIXES.

They help us form adjectives.
اللاحقة (Suffix) هي حرف أو مجموعة حروف نضيفها في نهاية الكلمة لتكوين كلمة جديدة.

مثال:
care = عناية / اهتمام
careful = حذر
careless = مهمل

نستعمل اللاحقتين -ful و -less لتكوين صفات تصف الأشخاص والمشاعر والسلوك.
LEARN · 02

🧠 Understanding -ful and -less

These two suffixes often create adjectives with contrasting meanings.

🌸 Suffix -FUL

The suffix -ful often means:

full of / having / showing

We add it to certain nouns to form adjectives.

care → careful
hope → hopeful
help → helpful
use → useful
thought → thoughtful
success → successful

Sequence 2 examples:

When I was younger, my mother was always helpful.

Today, I am hopeful about achieving my dreams.

My childhood was full of joyful moments.
اللاحقة -ful تدل غالباً على الامتلاك أو الاتصاف بشيء.

careful = حذر
helpful = متعاون / مساعد
hopeful = متفائل / مفعم بالأمل
useful = مفيد
successful = ناجح

نستعمل هذه الصفات لوصف الشخصية والمشاعر والتجارب.

💜 Suffix -LESS

The suffix -less often means:

without / lacking

We add it to certain nouns to form adjectives.

care → careless
hope → hopeless
help → helpless
use → useless
thought → thoughtless
fear → fearless

Sequence 2 examples:

When I was a child, I was sometimes careless with my school things.

After failing the competition, I felt hopeless.

My grandfather was fearless when facing difficulties.
اللاحقة -less تدل غالباً على غياب الشيء أو عدم امتلاكه.

careless = مهمل
hopeless = فاقد الأمل
helpless = عاجز / لا حول له
useless = عديم الفائدة
thoughtless = غير مراعي لمشاعر الآخرين
fearless = شجاع / لا يخاف

⚖️ Compare the Meanings

Base Word -FUL Adjective -LESS Adjective
care careful careless
hope hopeful hopeless
help helpful helpless
use useful useless
thought thoughtful thoughtless
meaning meaningful meaningless
harm harmful harmless
power powerful powerless
Important:

Words ending in -ful and -less are not always exact opposites in every situation.

For example:
helpful = willing or able to help.
helpless = unable to help yourself.

Their meanings are related, but they are not simple opposites.
LEARN · 03

⚠️ Spelling Rules & Exceptions

Learn how to spell adjectives correctly when adding -ful and -less.

1. The suffix -ful has only ONE L.

✓ helpful
✗ helpfull

✓ beautiful
✗ beautifull
2. The suffix -less has TWO letters S at the end.

✓ careless
✗ careles

✓ hopeless
✗ hopeles
3. Some base words change their spelling.

beauty → beautiful
pity → pitiful

The final y changes to i in these words.
4. Not every noun takes both suffixes.

successful is a common adjective.
But successless is not a standard everyday adjective.

Use unsuccessful instead.

Also:
beautiful is correct.
beautyless is not a standard adjective.

Use unattractive or another suitable adjective.
5. Some adjectives have special meanings.

fearless means very brave, not simply a person who has no fear at all.

priceless means extremely valuable, not necessarily worthless.

Always learn the meaning of the whole adjective.
قواعد مهمة:

1. اللاحقة -ful تنتهي بحرف L واحد فقط.
helpful وليس helpfull

2. اللاحقة -less تنتهي بحرفي S.
careless وليس careles

3. بعض الكلمات تتغير كتابتها:
beauty → beautiful

4. لا يمكن إضافة اللاحقتين إلى جميع الأسماء.

5. يجب حفظ معنى الصفة كاملة لأن المعنى قد يكون خاصاً.

✍️ My Childhood and Personality

Write a short paragraph describing your childhood personality, your memories, and how you have changed. Use at least three adjectives ending in -ful or -less.

Writing Guide:

1. Introduce your childhood personality.
2. Describe your childhood experiences.
3. Explain how you have changed.
4. Finish with a positive thought about your future.

Example: When I was younger, I was sometimes careless, but I was always cheerful.

Example: I had many wonderful memories with my helpful grandparents.

Example: Today, I am more careful and hopeful about my future.
