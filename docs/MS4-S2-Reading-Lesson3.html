📚 SIX PARAGRAPHS · ONE PERSONAL STORY

All About Me

01 My name is Adam, and I am fourteen years old. I am a middle-school pupil who lives with my family in a small town. People who know me well usually describe me as calm, friendly and hardworking. I am not always confident when I meet new people, but I become more talkative when I feel comfortable. I try to be honest with others, and I believe that being respectful is one of the most important qualities a person can have. My friends sometimes say that I am ambitious because I like setting goals and working hard to achieve them.
02 At school, English is one of my favourite subjects. I enjoy learning new words and expressing my ideas in English. I also like science because I am curious about how things work. Last year, I was not very confident when I had to speak in front of the class. While my classmates were preparing a presentation, I was worrying about making mistakes. My English teacher encouraged me to participate, and I slowly became more comfortable. I discovered that mistakes are not always negative; sometimes they help us learn.
03 Outside school, I have several hobbies. I enjoy playing football with my friends, reading adventure stories and listening to music. I also spend some time using digital devices, but I try not to spend the whole day online. My best friend and I often play football after school. Unlike me, he is very patient and prefers quiet activities such as reading and drawing. Whereas I usually make decisions quickly, he thinks carefully before choosing what to do. We are different, but these differences make our friendship interesting.
04 My family is also very important to me. My parents always encourage me to work hard and believe in myself. My older sister is especially helpful when I have a difficult school task. When I was younger, I sometimes thought that asking for help was a sign of weakness. One day, however, I had a difficult project and did not know how to organise my ideas. My sister helped me make a plan, and I finally completed the project successfully. That experience taught me that asking for help can be a strength, not a weakness.
05 I have also learned a lot from difficult experiences. Two years ago, I entered a school competition, but I did not win. At first, I felt disappointed and wanted to give up. Then my teacher told me that unsuccessful attempts can teach us valuable lessons. I listened to her advice and decided to try again. The following year, I prepared more carefully and won a prize. It was one of the most important experiences of my school life because it taught me to be determined.
06 In the future, I will continue working towards my goals. I would like to study at university and perhaps become an engineer. I know that the road will not always be easy, but I am ready to work hard. I want to become a responsible, independent and successful person who can help others. I also hope to keep my friendships and continue learning new things. I do not know exactly what the future will bring, but I know that my choices, experiences and efforts will help shape the person I become.
