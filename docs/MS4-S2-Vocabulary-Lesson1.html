THE MEMORY ALBUM

A Trip Back in Time 📸

When I was younger, I went to primary school with four children who became my closest friends. Their names were Sara, Yacine, Karim and Nadia.

We were different, but we always enjoyed being together. Today, when I look at this old school album, many wonderful memories come back to me.

“Our personalities were different, but our friendship made every school day special.”

02 / SARA RECOGNISE
🌼 Sara — The Calm Friend

I remember Sara very well. She was a calm and patient girl. When a classmate had a problem, Sara did not become angry. She listened carefully and tried to help.

She was also very kind and helpful. If someone forgot a pencil or did not understand an exercise, Sara was always ready to help.

Choose the Traits
Select the THREE words that describe Sara.
03 / YACINE UNDERSTAND
🎯 Yacine — The Dreamer

Yacine was different from Sara. He was very hardworking and serious. Sometimes he stayed after class because he wanted to finish his work perfectly.

Yacine was also ambitious. He often talked about his future and his dreams. When something was difficult, he was determined and continued trying.

Meaning from Context
Yacine continued working even when a task was difficult. Which adjective describes him best?
04 / KARIM UNDERSTAND
🍪 Karim — The Giving Friend

Karim was probably the most generous child in our group. He often shared his snacks, pencils and games with us.

He was also caring and friendly. When one of us felt sad, Karim noticed it and tried to make us smile again.

Match the Evidence
Match Karim's actions with his personality traits.
He shared his snacks.
He noticed when friends were sad.
He enjoyed talking to everyone.
05 / NADIA APPLY
🎤 Nadia — The Confident Friend

Nadia was a very self-confident girl. I remember how she stood in front of the class and spoke without being afraid.

She was also brave, outgoing and sociable. She loved meeting people and making new friends.

Complete the Memory
Choose the best adjectives.

Nadia was very because she could speak in front of the class.

She was also and was not afraid of new situations.

Nadia was very because she enjoyed meeting people.

07 / SHARED MEMORY ANALYSE
ONE SCHOOL DAY

Different Personalities, One Team

One afternoon, our teacher asked us to prepare a small presentation.

Sara calmly organised our materials. Yacine worked hard and made sure that everything was ready. Karim shared his pencils and helped anyone who needed support. Nadia stood in front of the class and presented our work confidently.

We all had different strengths, but together we made a great team.
