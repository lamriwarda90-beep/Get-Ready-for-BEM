THE FUTURE JOURNAL

A Dream Begins 🌟

One evening, a pupil sat at his desk and opened his notebook.

He started thinking about his future. He had a big dream and an important goal.

He wanted to build a successful career.

“Every great achievement begins with a dream and a plan.”
01 / DISCOVER REMEMBER

What Will I Become?

Discover the important words we use when talking about the future.

The pupil knew that achieving his dream would require a good plan, hard work and determination. His ambition motivated him to keep learning.

02 / DREAM JOB RECOGNISE
Exploring different professions
💼 My Dream Job

The pupil imagined different professions . Perhaps he could become a doctor and help people. Perhaps he could become an engineer and design useful things.

Maybe he could be a teacher, architect, pilot or programmer. Every job looked interesting. He needed to discover which dream job was right for him.

03 / EDUCATION UNDERSTAND
Studying for a better future
🎓 My Path to Higher Education

The pupil knew that some careers require higher education . He imagined himself at a university , studying in a library and learning new things.

He would need to study seriously and work towards a degree . Education could give him more knowledge and new opportunities .

04 / CAREER PATH UNDERSTAND
Making an important decision
🧭 Choosing My Career Path

There were many possible directions. He had to choose carefully. Making a choice about his future was not easy.

He thought about his interests, abilities and dreams. Finally, he understood that choosing a career path meant deciding what he wanted to do in the future.

05 / HARD WORK APPLY
Effort, determination and hard work
💪 Working Towards My Dream

The pupil knew that dreams do not come true by themselves. He needed to be hardworking and determined .

He studied every day, practised his skills and prepared for the future. He understood that effort could help him achieve his goal and eventually succeed .

hardworking مجتهد
determined مصمم / عازم
effort جهد
practise يتدرب
prepare يستعد
achieve يحقق
succeed ينجح

06 / SUCCESS APPLY
A successful future
🏆 I Achieved My Dream!

Years later, the pupil's hard work had paid off. He had achieved his goal. He had become a successful professional and was proud of his career .

His dream had become reality because he had studied, worked hard and never stopped believing in himself.

07 / REFLECT ANALYSE
Looking ahead with confidence
🌟 My Dream, My Future

The pupil closed his notebook and smiled. He knew that his dream would require confidence , patience and determination.

His future was still waiting for him. He had a clear goal and a plan. Now it was time to keep learning and move towards his dream.
