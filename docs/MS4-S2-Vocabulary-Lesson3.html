01
MEMORY MOMENT 01 · DISCOVER

📦 The Memory Box

🌱 LEVEL 1 · REMEMBER
📸 A box full of memories

Adam discovers the past

One afternoon, Adam was cleaning his room when he found an old box under his bed. It was full of photographs, school notebooks and little objects from his childhood.

Adam opened the box carefully. Each object brought back a different memory. He began to remember the experiences that had made his childhood special.

💡 Vocabulary Focus
A memory is something you remember from the past. An experience is something that happened to you.
memory childhood past remember experience
🔎

Meaning Detective

Read carefully and choose the correct meaning.

1. What does a “memory” refer to?

2. Which word means “something that happened to you”?

02
MEMORY MOMENT 02 · CHILDHOOD

🏫 My First Day at School

🌱 LEVEL 2 · UNDERSTAND
🎒 A new beginning

A new beginning

Adam remembers his first day at primary school. He was only six years old. He had a new schoolbag and was standing at the school entrance for the first time.

At first, he felt nervous because everything was new. Then his teacher smiled and welcomed him. Adam soon felt excited when he met his new classmates.

❤️ Feelings Focus
Nervous: worried about something new.
Excited: very happy and interested.
first day nervous excited classmate welcome
❤️

Feelings Check

03
MEMORY MOMENT 03 · ADVENTURE

🚌 A Special School Trip

🌿 LEVEL 3 · EXPLORE
🚌 An unforgettable adventure

An unforgettable adventure

A few years later, Adam went on a special school trip with his classmates. They visited an interesting place and discovered something new together.

Adam loved exploring the place and taking photographs. The trip was so exciting that he knew he would never forget it. It became one of his most unforgettable childhood experiences.

🔎 Word Detective
Unforgettable describes something so special that you cannot forget it.
trip explore discover exciting unforgettable
🧠

Choose the Best Meaning

04
MEMORY MOMENT 04 · ACHIEVEMENT

🏆 The Day I Felt Proud

🌿 LEVEL 4 · APPLY
🏆 A moment of success

A moment of success

Another photograph reminded Adam of a very special school day. He had worked hard for a class activity and finally succeeded.

His teacher gave him a certificate and congratulated him. His classmates applauded. Adam felt extremely proud because his hard work had led to an important achievement.

🏆 Connect the Ideas
Hard work → Achievement → Success → Feeling proud.
achievement succeed proud certificate congratulate
🏆

Cause & Effect

Why did Adam feel proud?

Which word means “something important that you successfully did”?

05
MEMORY MOMENT 05 · CHALLENGE

🌧️ A Difficult Experience

🌳 LEVEL 5 · ANALYSE
🌧️ Learning from mistakes

A mistake becomes a lesson

Not every memory was happy. Adam also remembered a difficult day when he made a mistake during a school activity.

At first, he felt disappointed and worried. However, his teacher encouraged him to try again. Adam understood that a mistake could become a lesson if he learned from it and tried to improve.

💡 Life Lesson
A difficult experience is not always a bad experience. Sometimes it teaches us an important lesson.
difficult mistake disappointed encourage lesson improve
🔄

Complete the Thought

Adam made a mistake, but he learned from it. Which conclusion is best?

MEMORY MOMENT 06 · EMOTIONS

🎉 A Happy Memory

🌳 LEVEL 6 · CLASSIFY
🎉 Happiness and friendship

A moment shared with friends

Adam smiled when he found another photograph. It showed him and his classmates during a joyful school celebration.

Everyone was laughing and enjoying the moment together. Adam remembers feeling joyful because he was surrounded by good friends. For him, friendship made the experience even more special.

❤️ Vocabulary Connection
Joyful, happy, laugh, friendship, celebration and together can all describe a positive shared experience.
joyful happy laugh friendship celebration together

MEMORY MOMENT 07 · REFLECT

💭 Looking Back

🌳 LEVEL 7 · THINK DEEPER
💭 Memories shape our lives

Memories shape who we are

Adam put the photographs back into the box. He had remembered happy moments, difficult moments, achievements and unforgettable adventures.

Looking at his childhood, Adam realized that every experience had taught him something. Some memories made him smile, while others helped him become stronger and more confident.

🌟 Big Idea
Our experiences can teach us lessons, influence our feelings and help us grow.
remember realize experience lesson grow confident
