🔗 LANGUAGE TOOLKIT · COHESION
🔗
STEP 05 · LINK YOUR IDEAS

Make your writing flow

Linking words connect your sentences and help readers follow your ideas naturally.

and Add information
because Give a reason
but Show contrast
whereas Compare differences
when Introduce a time or event
while Describe simultaneous actions
🔗 Mission 05 — Choose the correct linker
1. I am talkative, ______ my friend is usually quiet.
2. We became close ______ we discovered that we lived in the same neighbourhood.
3. Samir was helping me ______ I was collecting my books.
4. I trusted him ______ he always kept my secrets.
💡

Remember: Because explains a reason, whereas compares differences, and while can describe two actions happening at the same time.

⏳ LANGUAGE TOOLKIT · GRAMMAR
⏳
STEP 06 · BRING MEMORIES TO LIFE

Choose the right past tense

Use the Past Simple for completed events and the Past Continuous for actions in progress at a particular moment in the past.

PAST SIMPLE

Samir helped me yesterday.

PAST CONTINUOUS + PAST SIMPLE

I was walking home when I fell.

⏰ Mission 06 — Complete the memories
1. We ______ when the teacher called us inside.
2. We ______ in the same neighbourhood during our childhood.
3. I ______ on the wet pavement when I slipped.
4. Samir ______ to help me after I fell.
⚖️ Bonus — Compare two personalities

I am more talkative, Samir usually thinks before he speaks.

📖 Study a Model Paragraph

MODEL · MY BEST CHILDHOOD FRIEND

My best childhood friend is Samir. We met at primary school when we were seven years old. He was cheerful, patient and generous. Unlike me, he was rather quiet, but we enjoyed playing football together. I still remember one rainy afternoon. While I was walking home, I slipped and fell. Samir immediately came to help me. I felt grateful because he was always there for me. That experience showed me the meaning of true friendship. I will always remember his kindness.

🎨

Colour guide: Pink = introduction, purple = description, yellow = memory, green = conclusion.

STEP 09 · YOUR FINAL MASTERPIECE

Become a Friendship Storyteller

It is time to write independently. Use everything you have learned to produce a coherent and meaningful paragraph.

💌 Your Final Writing Mission

Write a paragraph of approximately 80–120 words about your best childhood friend. You may describe a real friend or an imaginary one.

  • Introduce your friend.
  • Explain how you met.
  • Use at least three personality adjectives.
  • Mention activities you shared.
  • Describe one special memory.
  • Use appropriate past tenses.
  • Include linking words and a comparison.
  • Express your feelings.
  • Finish with a meaningful conclusion.
