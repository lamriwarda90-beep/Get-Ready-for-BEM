Read a Model Paragraph

📖 Read the paragraph about an ideal teacher. اقرأ الفقرة ثم لاحظ كيف تم تنظيم الأفكار.

💡 Reading tip: The colours show the different parts of the paragraph. You do not need to memorise the text.

🌷 My Ideal Teacher

My ideal teacher is Mrs Amina, my English teacher.

She is kind, patient and helpful. She always smiles and respects her pupils.

She explains lessons clearly and helps us when we have problems. For example, when I make a mistake, she encourages me to try again.

I admire her because she makes learning easy and enjoyable. For me, she is an ideal teacher.

1. Introduction

Introduce the teacher.

2. Qualities

Describe the teacher's personality.

3. Actions and Example

Explain what the teacher does.

4. Conclusion

Give your final opinion.

🧠 Check Your Understanding

1. Who is Mrs Amina?
2. How many qualities are mentioned in the second sentence?
3. What does Mrs Amina do when a pupil makes a mistake?

📝 Helpful Sentence Starters

Use these expressions:

1. Introduction: My ideal teacher is ...

2. Qualities: He / She is ... , ... and ...

3. Actions: He / She always ...

4. Example: For example, when I ..., he / she ...

5. Conclusion: I admire him / her because ...
