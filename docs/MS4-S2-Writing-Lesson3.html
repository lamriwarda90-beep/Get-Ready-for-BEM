Mission 04 · Grammar Workshop

Choose the Right Tense

A personal profile describes your present life, remembers past experiences and explains future plans.

🟢 Present Simple I study English.
I enjoy reading.
🟡 Past Simple I visited Algiers last year.
I won a school competition.
🟣 Past Continuous I was studying when my friend called.
🔵 Future Simple I will work hard.
I will achieve my dream.

🧠 Grammar Challenge

Choose the correct verb form.

1. I ______ English every day.
2. Last year, I ______ a school trip.
3. I ______ a doctor in the future.
4. I enjoy ______ stories.
5. I ______ my homework when the lights went out.
6. I ______ very shy when I was younger.

06
Mission 06 · Connect Your Ideas

Make Your Writing Flow

Connectors make your personal profile smoother and help readers understand your ideas.

➕ Addition and · also · moreover · in addition
💡 Reason because · since
⚖️ Contrast but · however · although
⏳ Time when · last year · in the future · finally

🎯 Choose the Best Connector

1. I am shy, ______ I am friendly.
2. I love English ______ it is interesting.
3. I enjoy drawing. ______, I like reading.
4. ______, I want to become an engineer.
5. I was very happy ______ I won the competition.

✍️ Use Connectors in Your Own Writing

Write three connected sentences about your school life and hobbies.

I enjoy studying English because I love learning new words. In addition, I like reading short stories. However, I sometimes find grammar difficult.
07
Mission 07 · Paragraph Architect

Organise Your Profile

A good personal profile follows a logical order. Start with your identity and finish with your future ambitions.

PART 01

🌸 Introduction

Name, age, hometown, school and personality.

PART 02

📚 Development

School life, hobbies, preferences and experiences.

PART 03

🚀 Conclusion

Ambitions, future plans and a positive ending.

🧩 Put the Sentences in Order

Use the arrows to organise the sentences into a logical personal profile.

Writing tip: A profile is not just a list of facts. Group similar ideas together and use connectors to link them.

Mission 08 · Writing Studio

Plan Your Personal Profile

You are almost ready! Use your notes to prepare the three parts of your final writing.

📝 Part 1 — Introduction

Introduce yourself and describe your personality.

My name is ... . I am ... years old, and I live in ... . I am a ... and ... person.

📚 Part 2 — School, Hobbies and Experiences

Talk about your school life, interests and one past experience.

My favourite subject is ... because ... . In my free time, I enjoy ... . Last year, I ... .

🚀 Part 3 — My Future

Describe your ambitions and how you plan to achieve them.

In the future, I hope to become ... . I will work hard because ... .
