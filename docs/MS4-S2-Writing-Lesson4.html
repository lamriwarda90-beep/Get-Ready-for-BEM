STEP 06 · LANGUAGE TOOLBOX

Grammar for Biographies

Learn the grammar you need before writing.

🟣 Past Simple

We use the Past Simple for completed actions.

He published Soliloques in 1946.

He worked as a journalist.

🌸 Past Passive

We use: was / were + past participle

He was born in 1929.

Nedjma was published in 1956.

🧠 Activity 6 — Choose the Correct Form

1. Kateb Yacine ______ in Constantine.

2. He ______ in demonstrations in 1945.

3. Nedjma ______ in 1956.

4. Kateb Yacine ______ in France in 1989.

Remember:

was born — used to talk about birth.

participated — regular Past Simple.

was published — Past Passive.

died — Past Simple of die.

STEP 07 · GUIDED WRITING

Let's Build a Biography

Write one small part at a time. Use the helpful sentence starters.

💡 For weak learners: You do not need to write everything at once. Complete the four small writing boxes.

🌟 Part 1 — Introduction

Write his name, nationality, profession, birthplace, and birth year.

Help:
Kateb Yacine was a famous Algerian ...
He was born in ... in ...

📚 Part 2 — Education and Early Life

Mention his education and the events of 1945.

Help:
He studied at ...
In 1945, he ...
He was ...

📖 Part 3 — Literary Career

Mention Soliloques, Nedjma, and his work as a journalist.

Help:
In 1946, he published ...
Later, he worked ...
In 1956, his famous novel ...

🏆 Part 4 — Later Life and Legacy

Mention his later work, award, death, and importance.

Help:
Later, he developed ...
In 1987, he received ...
He died in ...
Today, he is remembered as ...
Example of a short biography:
Kateb Yacine was a famous Algerian writer, poet, journalist and playwright. He was born in Constantine in 1929.

He studied at a Quranic school, a French school and later in Sétif. In 1945, he participated in demonstrations and was arrested.

In 1946, he published his poetry collection Soliloques. Later, he worked as a journalist at Alger Républicain. His famous novel Nedjma was published in 1956. Another important work, Le Polygone étoilé, appeared in 1966.

From 1971, he developed popular theatre in Algerian Arabic. In 1987, he received a literary award in France. He died on 28 October 1989 in France. Today, he is remembered as an important figure in Algerian literature.

Use this model to learn how a biography is organised. Try to write your own sentences.
