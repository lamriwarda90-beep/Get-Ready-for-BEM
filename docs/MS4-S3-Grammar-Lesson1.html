Discover the Language

Hint: It is a verb, but there is no written subject such as you.
💡 What do you notice?
Keep, Stay, Help, Respect, Take and Be are verbs in the base form. We normally do not write you before the verb. This form is called the imperative.
صيغة الأمر في الإنجليزية تبدأ بالفعل في صورته الأصلية، دون كتابة الفاعل عادةً.
مثال: Keep the classroom clean.

STEP 02 · LEARN THE RULE

The Imperative Toolkit

Use these four grammar patterns to give instructions, recommendations and advice.

✅ Affirmative Imperative

Use the base form of the verb. The subject you is understood.

BASE VERB + ...
Keep the streets clean.
Respect public places.
Help people in need.
Protect the environment.

🚫 Negative Imperative

Use don't followed by the base form of the verb.

DON'T + BASE VERB
Don't throw rubbish.
Don't waste water.
Don't damage public property.
Don't cross carelessly.

🔵 Imperative with “Be”

Use be to recommend behaviour or a person's state.

BE + ADJECTIVE
Be careful.
Be respectful.
Be responsible.
Don't be careless.

💡 Should / Shouldn't

Should gives advice. Shouldn't says something is not a good idea.

SUBJECT + SHOULD(N'T) + BASE VERB
You should recycle.
Citizens should help others.
You shouldn't litter.
We shouldn't waste water.
🎯 BEM Grammar Alert
After should / shouldn't, use the base verb.

✓ You should respect the rules.
✗ You should respects the rules.
✗ You should to respect the rules.
✗ You should respecting the rules.
الأمر المثبت: الفعل في المصدر دون to.
الأمر المنفي: Don't + الفعل في المصدر.
النصيحة: should / shouldn't + الفعل في المصدر.
STEP 03 · COMPARE

Imperative or Should?

Both can give advice. Notice how the sentence structure changes.

🟣 Imperative

A direct recommendation. The subject you is understood.

BASE VERB
Keep the park clean.

🟢 Should

Advice about what is a good idea. A subject is written.

SUBJECT + SHOULD + BASE VERB
You should keep the park clean.
🧠 Memory Trick:
KEEP the park clean. → Direct recommendation.
YOU SHOULD KEEP the park clean. → Advice.
