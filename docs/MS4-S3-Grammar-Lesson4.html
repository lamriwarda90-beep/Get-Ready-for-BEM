Discover the Rule

What are discourse markers?

🏘️ Community Topic Choose a problem or an issue.
🔗 Discourse Marker Connect your ideas logically.
💬 Strong Argument Explain your opinion clearly.
💡 Remember!

Discourse markers are words or expressions that help us organise and connect ideas in speaking and writing.


First, citizens should keep public places clean.

Moreover, they should respect their neighbours.

Finally, everyone should help improve the community.

أدوات الربط هي كلمات وعبارات نستعملها لتنظيم الأفكار وربط الحجج ببعضها. تساعدنا على كتابة فقرة واضحة ومتسلسلة.

03

Read a Model Argument

Notice how the connectors organise the paragraph.

🌍 How Can Young People Help Their Community?

Young people can play an important role in making their community better.

First, they can help keep their neighbourhood clean.

Moreover, they can support elderly neighbours.

For example, pupils can organise clean-up campaigns and collect clothes for people in need.

However, some young people may have little free time.

Therefore, schools can organise short volunteering activities.

Finally, everyone should remember that even small actions can improve community life.

🌟
Writing Secret
A good argumentative paragraph includes a clear opinion, supporting arguments, examples and a concluding idea.

Challenge 5 — Connect the Ideas

Rewrite and combine sentences.

TASK 05

Join the ideas using the marker in brackets.

1. Citizens should keep streets clean. They should protect public parks.
(Moreover)
2. Community activities are useful. Some people have little free time.
(However)
3. Young people can help their community. They can organise a clean-up day.
(For example)
4. The neighbourhood has a lot of rubbish. Residents should organise a clean-up campaign.
(Therefore)
5. Pupils can support their community. They can help elderly neighbours.
(In addition)
6. Citizens have different responsibilities. They should work together.
(Finally)
Possible answers:

1. Citizens should keep streets clean. Moreover, they should protect public parks.
2. Community activities are useful. However, some people have little free time.
3. Young people can help their community. For example, they can organise a clean-up day.
4. The neighbourhood has a lot of rubbish. Therefore, residents should organise a clean-up campaign.
5. Pupils can support their community. In addition, they can help elderly neighbours.
6. Citizens have different responsibilities. Finally, they should work together.

For example → Example
However → Contrast
Therefore → Result
