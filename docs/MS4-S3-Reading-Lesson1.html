How to Be a Good Citizen

01 • BEFORE READING

🔮 Look & Predict

02 • READ

📖 Read the Text

A Good Citizen Starts with You

An article written for the school citizenship club

01

What makes a person a good citizen? Is it enough to love your country? A good citizen does more than say kind words. Good citizens respect other people, follow rules and take care of their community. They know that everyone has rights, but everyone also has responsibilities. If we all do our part, our neighbourhoods will become safer and happier places.

02

Start with your surroundings. Keep your classroom, school and streets clean. Never throw rubbish on the ground. Put it in a bin and encourage your friends to do the same. If you see litter in the playground, pick it up safely and put it in the right place. Save water, switch off unnecessary lights and protect public property. These small actions can make a big difference.

03

Good citizens also care about people. Be polite to your neighbours and respect older people. Offer help to someone who needs it. If an elderly neighbour needs help carrying shopping bags, offer a hand. If a new pupil feels lonely, invite them to join your group. Listen to different opinions and never make fun of others. Kindness and respect help everyone feel welcome.

04

Last month, pupils from our middle school organised a community day. They cleaned a small park, planted flowers and collected books for children who needed them. At first, only ten pupils volunteered. Soon, more classmates joined. By the end of the day, the park looked beautiful and the children had received many books. The pupils discovered that working together can change lives.

05

You do not need to be famous or rich to help your country. Begin with one useful action today. Respect the rules, speak kindly and help whenever you can. If every young person takes responsibility, our community will grow stronger. Remember: citizenship is not only about what you receive; it is also about what you give.

🌱 Every small action matters.
