A Little Kindness Goes a Long Way

A story from a middle-school charity club

💜
01

Last winter, a group of pupils from El Amal Middle School noticed that some children in their town did not have enough warm clothes or school supplies. The pupils felt sad, but they decided to do something useful. They formed a small charity club called Helping Hands. Their motto was simple: “A little kindness can make a big difference.”

02

First, the club members organised a donation campaign. They asked classmates, teachers and parents to donate clean clothes, notebooks, pens and storybooks. They placed colourful collection boxes near the school entrance. They also made posters to encourage more people to participate. Within one week, the boxes were full of donations.

03

Next, the volunteers sorted the donated items and prepared small gift packages. Some pupils folded clothes while others packed school supplies. Their teachers helped them check that everything was clean and useful. On Saturday morning, the volunteers delivered the packages to a local community centre. They wanted to support families who needed help.

04

At the centre, the pupils met several children and gave them the packages. One little girl smiled when she received a warm coat and a storybook. “Thank you! I can read a new story tonight!” she said happily. The volunteers felt proud because their small efforts had brought happiness to others. They realised that charity is not only about giving things; it is also about sharing love, time and attention.

05

After this experience, the club decided to organise more activities. The pupils planned to collect books, visit elderly people and clean public spaces. Their teacher encouraged them to continue. “If we work together, we will help more people,” she said. The pupils understood that everyone can make a positive change, even through small actions.

✨ Kindness becomes powerful when people work together.

09 • NOTICE HOW LANGUAGE WORKS

🧠 Language in the Story

Discover useful language through the text.

PAST SIMPLE

What happened?

We use the past simple to talk about completed past actions.

The pupils collected donations.
They gave the children gifts.

نستعمل الماضي البسيط للحديث عن أحداث وقعت وانتهت في الماضي.

FIRST CONDITIONAL

What will happen?

We use the first conditional to talk about a possible future result.

If we work together, we will help more people.

If + present simple, will + base verb

نستعمل الشرط الأول للتعبير عن نتيجة محتملة في المستقبل.

EXPRESSING PURPOSE

Why did they do it?

We use to or in order to to explain the purpose of an action.

They made posters to encourage people.
They collected clothes in order to help families.

نستعمل هذه العبارات للتعبير عن الهدف من القيام بفعل معين.
