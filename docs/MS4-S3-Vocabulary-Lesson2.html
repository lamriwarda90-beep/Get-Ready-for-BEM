The “Warm Hearts” Project

Last winter, a group of pupils decided to help families in their neighbourhood. They called their project “Warm Hearts”. First, they collected warm clothes and blankets. Then, they organised a small fundraising event at school. Some pupils prepared posters, while others talked to parents and neighbours about the project. The collected items were given to families who needed support.

1. What was the name of the project?

2. What did the pupils collect?

3. Who received the collected items?
