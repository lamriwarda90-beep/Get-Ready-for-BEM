We Can Do It! ✨

You do not need difficult English. Use short, clear sentences. Every step will help you write your letter.

01

Read the situation 🎯

Situation of Integration — Writing Task
Your school is organising a solidarity campaign to help poor families in your town. Some families need clothes, food and school supplies.

You want your schoolmates to participate. Write an open letter (about 70–90 words) for your school noticeboard. Encourage pupils to donate useful things and help families in need.

Use: a greeting, the problem, two or three suggestions, and a polite ending.
الوضعية: مدرستك تنظم حملة تضامن لمساعدة العائلات المحتاجة. اكتب رسالة مفتوحة بسيطة لتشجيع زملائك على التبرع والمشاركة.

🔎 Check that you understand

1. Who will read your letter?

2. Why are you writing?

02

Discover the letter 📨

An open letter has different parts. Discover what each part does. You do not need to read a complete letter yet!

💌 Greeting

We begin our letter with a greeting.

BEGINNING Dear schoolmates,
التحية في بداية الرسالة

💬 Problem

We explain why we are writing.

PROBLEM Some families need our help.
شرح المشكلة

🤝 Suggestions

We suggest helpful actions.

ACTION We can donate clothes.
تقديم اقتراحات للمساعدة

🌟 Ending

We encourage people to participate.

CALL TO ACTION Please join us!
تشجيع الآخرين على المشاركة

🧩 Match each expression to its job

03

Collect easy words 🧺

These words will help you. Read them aloud, then choose useful ideas for your letter.

🎁 Things to donate

clothes 👕 food 🍞 books 📚 schoolbags 🎒 pens ✏️

🤝 Helpful actions

donate collect share help join

💬 Choose two helpful actions

مفردات مفيدة: donate = يتبرع، collect = يجمع، clothes = ملابس، school supplies = أدوات مدرسية، join = ينضم.

05

Put it all together ✍️

Click the button to create a first draft from your sentences. Then edit it to make it your own. You may add one more example or a kind message.

Your chosen sentences give you a starting point. Read and change the words you want. Your letter should be about 70–90 words.

💡 Need one more sentence?

Choose a useful extra sentence and add it to your letter:

Every donation is important. Helping others makes us happy. We can work together.
06

Check & celebrate! 🏆
