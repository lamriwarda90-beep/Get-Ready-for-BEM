01

Read the situation 🎯

Situation of Integration — Writing Task

Your school magazine is preparing a special issue about Algerian culture and identity. Some teenagers spend a lot of time on social media and forget their local traditions.

You believe that young people should protect their national culture.

Write a short article (about 80–100 words) for your school magazine. Express your opinion, give two reasons, support them with examples, and explain how young people can protect Algerian culture.

Use: a title, an opinion, two arguments, examples, and a conclusion.
الوضعية: تُحضّر مجلة مدرستك عددًا خاصًا بالثقافة والهوية الجزائرية. اكتب مقالًا قصيرًا تعبر فيه عن رأيك حول أهمية الحفاظ على ثقافتنا، مع تقديم سببين وأمثلة وخاتمة.

02

Discover the article 🧩

An argumentative article gives an opinion and supports it with reasons and examples. Discover its parts using short expressions.

💬 1. Opinion

Tell the reader what you think.

OPINION I think our culture is important.
التعبير عن الرأي

💡 2. Reason

Explain why you think so.

REASON It connects us to our history.
تقديم سبب أو حجة

🇩🇿 3. Example

Give a real example.

EXAMPLE For example, traditional clothes.
تقديم مثال

🌟 4. Conclusion

Finish with a clear message.

CONCLUSION We should protect our heritage.
الخاتمة
✨ Useful order: Title → Opinion → Reason 1 → Example → Reason 2 → Example → Conclusion
