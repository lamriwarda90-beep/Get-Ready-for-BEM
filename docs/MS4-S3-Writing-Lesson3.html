Every Good Action Counts! ✨

Follow six simple steps to write an open letter. Choose your ideas, build your sentences, and encourage others to be good citizens.

01

Read the situation 🎯

Situation of Integration — Writing Task

Your school is organising a Good Citizenship Week. You notice that some people throw litter in the streets, damage public places, and do not help their neighbours.

You want to encourage your schoolmates to become responsible citizens.

Write an open letter (about 70–90 words) for your school noticeboard. Explain why good citizenship is important, suggest three positive actions, and encourage your schoolmates to participate.

Use: a greeting, the problem, suggestions, and a polite ending.
الوضعية: تنظم مدرستك أسبوع المواطنة الصالحة. لاحظت أن بعض الأشخاص يرمون النفايات ويتلفون الأماكن العامة ولا يساعدون الآخرين. اكتب رسالة مفتوحة لتشجيع زملائك على أن يكونوا مواطنين صالحين، مع تقديم ثلاثة اقتراحات وخاتمة مناسبة.

🔎 Check that you understand

1. Who will read your letter?

2. Why are you writing?

02

Discover the letter 📨

An open letter has different parts. Discover each part through short expressions. You do not need to read a complete letter.

💌 Greeting

Begin by addressing your readers.

GREETING Dear schoolmates,
التحية الافتتاحية

⚠️ Problem

Explain what needs to change.

PROBLEM Our streets need to be cleaner.
عرض المشكلة

🤝 Suggestions

Tell people what they can do.

SUGGESTION We should put litter in bins.
تقديم الاقتراحات

🌟 Ending

Encourage people to take action.

ENDING Let's work together!
التشجيع والخاتمة
✨ Remember the order: Greeting → Problem → Suggestions → Call to action → Sign-off
