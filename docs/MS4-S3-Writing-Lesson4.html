What Do You Think? ✨

There is no single correct opinion. Choose your position, support it with reasons, and write in clear English.

01

Read the situation 🎯

Situation of Integration — Writing Task

Your school magazine is preparing a special issue called “Teenagers and Social Media.” Some pupils say that social media helps them learn and communicate. Others think it wastes time and creates problems.

Write a short argumentative article (about 80–100 words) for your school magazine. Answer the question: “Is social media good for teenagers?” Express your opinion, give two reasons with examples, mention one possible disadvantage or advantage, and conclude with advice.

Use: a title, opinion, arguments, examples, linking words and conclusion.
الوضعية: تُحضّر مجلة مدرستك موضوعًا حول المراهقين ووسائل التواصل الاجتماعي. اكتب مقالًا حجاجيًا قصيرًا تعبر فيه عن رأيك: هل وسائل التواصل الاجتماعي مفيدة أم ضارة؟ قدم حجتين مع أمثلة، واذكر وجهة نظر أخرى، ثم اختم بنصيحة.

02

Discover the article 🧩

An argumentative article expresses an opinion and supports it with reasons and examples. It can also mention a different point of view.

💬 Opinion

Say what you think.

OPINION I think social media can be useful.
التعبير عن الرأي

💡 Reason

Explain why you think so.

REASON It helps us communicate.
تقديم حجة أو سبب

🔎 Example

Support your reason with an example.

EXAMPLE For example, we can contact relatives.
تقديم مثال

⚖️ Contrast

Mention a different point of view.

CONTRAST However, it can waste our time.
ذكر وجهة نظر مختلفة
✨ Article structure: Title → Opinion → Reason 1 → Example → Reason 2 → Example → Contrast → Conclusion

03

Collect easy words 🧺

Social media has advantages and disadvantages. Explore both sides before choosing your opinion.

👍 Advantages

learn online communicate share ideas make friends find information watch lessons
الإيجابيات: التعلم، التواصل، مشاركة الأفكار والحصول على المعلومات.

👎 Disadvantages

waste time distraction false information privacy problems cyberbullying too much screen time
السلبيات: تضييع الوقت، التشتت، الأخبار الكاذبة ومشكلات الخصوصية.
