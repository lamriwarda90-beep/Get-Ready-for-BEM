🧭 Your Writing Route

Follow these four steps to become a confident writer.

💡

1. PLAN

Understand the topic, audience and purpose. Collect useful ideas before writing.

🧩

2. ORGANISE

Arrange your ideas into an introduction, body and conclusion.

🔗

3. SUPPORT

Give reasons and examples. Use linking words to connect ideas.

🔍

4. REVIEW

Check spelling, punctuation, grammar and organisation.
